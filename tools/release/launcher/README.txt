LIFE FRAMEWORK PLAYTEST
=======================

How to play
-----------
1. You need Arma Reforger from Steam, installed and up to date.
2. Double-click "Play Life Framework".
3. Pick one:
   - Play on my own: the playtest town, just you.
   - Join the community server: play with others when someone is hosting.
     Sessions are announced on Discord.

The game takes a minute or two to load the town.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
If it asks whether to open the file, click "Run" or "Open".

Keep this folder together: "Play Life Framework" needs the "addons" and
"launcher" folders next to it. You can move the whole folder anywhere.

Problems or feedback
--------------------
Discord:  https://discord.gg/MjMexJteqz
Website:  https://life-framework.org/playtest

The launcher writes what it did to:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\LifeFramework\launcher.log
Attach that file when you report a problem with starting the game.

Getting a newer build
---------------------
Download the zip again from https://life-framework.org and replace this folder.
Your characters on the community server are kept on the server.
