## Philosophy & Vision

We're a group of passionate modders contributing our time because we love life mods and want to bring back the vibrant life server community we all enjoyed. This is our passion project – built for fun, built to share, and built to play.

We ship fast and accept new contributors with open arms. Whether you're an experienced developer or just getting started, we want your input, your ideas, and your code. This is a community effort, and everyone has something valuable to contribute.

Our commitment goes beyond just this mod. We're contributing to the community through shared learnings, documentation, and knowledge. When we solve problems or discover better approaches, we share them so everyone benefits.

- **Server owners** to mix and match features to create their unique experience
- **A thriving ecosystem** where community creations work together harmoniously

Whether you want to add new jobs, custom economies, unique vehicle systems, or entirely new gameplay mechanics, Life Framework provides the structure to make your vision a reality without starting from scratch.

## About the Base Mod

### Set in 1989 for optimal asset utilization

The Life Framework base mod is set in 1989 to make the best use of Arma Reforger's native assets and setting. This period perfectly aligns with the available vehicles, weapons, and environments, providing a rich and immersive foundation out of the box.

However, we understand that every server owner has a unique vision. The framework is designed to be time-period agnostic at its core, which means:

- You can deploy additional asset mods to shift your server to any era (modern, WW2, futuristic, etc.)
- The core systems (economy, jobs, interactions) work regardless of the time period
- Mix and match different asset packs to create your perfect blend

The 1989 base simply ensures that everyone can start with a complete, working experience using only Arma Reforger's built-in content. From there, the sky's the limit for customization with community mods and asset packs.
