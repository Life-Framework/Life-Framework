# Island garage (#3363): buy a car at the Saint-Philippe lot, store it at the garage pad, retrieve it, and
# reach the impound desk, on real Everon (MainWorld). Needs a fresh client profile (quiz, form R-89, $100 cash,
# $1000 bank).
#
#   node agent-box/session.mjs up --mission island --clients 1 --roster 1 --profile lowspec --wait
#   node agent-box/harness.mjs --client 1 flow island-garage
#   node agent-box/session.mjs down
#
# Read the server console for [LFDebug:Vehicle] after the run. `park` stands in for driving the bought car to the
# nearest garage (#348).

ui
click QuizOption1Button
wait 500
click QuizOption0Button
wait 500
click QuizOption2Button
wait 500
click QuizOption1Button
wait 500
click QuizOption0Button
wait 2500
click QuizContinueButton
wait 2500
type RegFirstName Ada
type RegLastName Newcomer
type RegAge 24
click SignButton
wait 8000

step 1 Withdraw $700 at the bank
stand Fit_saint_philippe_bank_bank_teller
wait 1500
use LF_ATMAction Fit_saint_philippe_bank_bank_teller
wait 1200
click Key7Button
click Key0Button
click Key0Button
click WithdrawButton
wait 1500
click CloseButton
wait 500
expect cash +700

step 2 Buy the cheapest car at the lot
stand Fit_saint_philippe_carlot_vehicle_lot
wait 1500
use LF_VehicleBuyAction Fit_saint_philippe_carlot_vehicle_lot
wait 1500
click Car0_Action
wait 2500
click LotClose
wait 500
expect money down
