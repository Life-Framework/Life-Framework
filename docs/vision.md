# Philosophy & Vision

> Source: https://life-framework.org/philosophy (scraped). Companion to
> [roadmap.md](./roadmap.md) and [design-principles.md](./design-principles.md).

We're a group of passionate modders contributing our time because we love life
mods and want to bring back the vibrant life server community we all enjoyed.
This is our passion project — built for fun, built to share, and built to play.

## Our Vision

We're building a **base framework with all the elements you need to create your
own unique Life experience**. Think of it as your foundation — all the core
systems, mechanics, and tools are ready to go, so you can focus on what makes
your server special.

We ship fast and accept new contributors with open arms. Whether you're an
experienced developer or just getting started, we want your input, your ideas,
and your code. This is a community effort, and everyone has something valuable
to contribute.

Our commitment goes beyond just this mod. We contribute to the community through
shared learnings, documentation, and knowledge. When we solve problems or
discover better approaches, we share them so everyone benefits.

We're also building an **easy-to-setup, playable version right out of the box**.
That's why the base mod is set in **1989** — to make the best use of Arma
Reforger's native assets, ensuring a complete, working experience from day one.

## Free & Open for Everyone

**MIT Licensed.** Life Framework is released under the MIT License, completely
free to use for any purpose — hobby server or commercial operation. No
restrictions, no fees, no barriers. Whether you're a solo modder, a small
community server, or a large commercial gaming network, Life Framework is yours
to use, modify, and build upon freely.

## Built for Extensibility

A standard foundation for unlimited customization. Life Framework provides a
standardized foundation that makes it easy for developers to create extensions,
plugins, and modifications. It's the *common language* that lets different
systems work together seamlessly.

By establishing clear standards and interfaces, we enable:

- Third-party developers to build compatible extensions without modifying the core
- Server owners to mix and match features to create their unique experience
- A thriving ecosystem where community creations work together harmoniously

Whether you want to add new jobs, custom economies, unique vehicle systems, or
entirely new gameplay mechanics, Life Framework provides the structure to make
your vision a reality without starting from scratch.

## About the Base Mod

### Set in 1989 for optimal asset utilization

The base mod is set in 1989 to make the best use of Arma Reforger's native
assets and setting. This period aligns with the available vehicles, weapons, and
environments, providing a rich and immersive foundation out of the box.

However, every server owner has a unique vision. The framework is designed to be
**time-period agnostic at its core**:

- Deploy additional asset mods to shift your server to any era (modern, WW2,
  futuristic, etc.)
- Core systems (economy, jobs, interactions) work regardless of the time period
- Mix and match different asset packs to create your perfect blend

The 1989 base ensures everyone can start with a complete, working experience
using only Arma Reforger's built-in content. From there, the sky's the limit for
customization with community mods and asset packs.

## Core Values

- **Open Source First** — The framework is built by the community, for the community.
- **Passion Driven** — We do this because it's fun and cool; we love life mods and want to play them again.
- **Accessible to All** — Run it on a massive server, with friends, or just locally. Your server, your rules, your way.
- **Move Fast** — Ship fast, iterate quickly, get features into players' hands. No bureaucracy, no roadblocks.
