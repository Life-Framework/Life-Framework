### Police
- **What it is:** Player-run faction you apply for. Nine ranks with salary paid every 5 min on duty (about 4k to 21k per payment), plus seven independent qualifications (traffic, customs, undercover, SWAT, air, academy, internal affairs). Every offence has a fixed fine set by law, so officers choose the charge, not the price; charges expire on their own after 1 to 3 days. Two ways to settle: an on-the-spot ticket from pocket cash, or paying the whole list from the bank. Jail wipes the inventory, and sentences run only while online. Tools: handcuffs, vehicle/person searches with per-item find chances, taser, spike strips, speed cameras, plate lookup, impound, in-car radar, dispatch with unit status codes, an encrypted radio channel, exclusion zones, house raids. Rank gates armoury and fleet.
- **Our status:** Mostly built. `LF_Jail` (timed sentence, escort, jailbreak #235), `LF_FineTickets` (paper ticket paid at the desk, #104), `LF_WantedBoard`/`LF_WantedReports`, `LF_Dispatch` plus dispatch board (#105, #250), `LF_PoliceRanks` (two ranks, armoury draw), `LF_DutyPay`, `LF_Contraband`/`LF_ConfiscateAction`, `LF_SpikeStrip`, garage and impound (#101), witnesses and evidence (#266-#269). Gaps: more than two ranks, rank-scaled pay, qualifications, shared kill-share of fines, checkpoints/exclusion zones, undercover. Speed cameras, tasers, aircraft are marked "not a fit" in m5-outline; police applications are M4 (#128, #314); crime catalog is M3 (#685); disconnect rules for cuffed/jailed players are #691.
- **Framework:** Rank ladder as config (N ranks, pay, gear and vehicle gates) and optional qualification tags that unlock named station kit. Fixed offence catalog (already planned as #685). Fine share split to on-duty officers as a config toggle. Per-item search find chance, with a hideable-compartment modifier.
- **Flagship:** 1989 gendarmerie ranks and pay; station gear drawn from base-game kit; a plain cruiser fleet; paper "wanted board" and radio calls instead of phone apps; road checkpoint kit (cones, barrier, spike strip, #646).
- **Fit and priority:** High for the rank/pay/qualification schema, because it shapes persisted data and so belongs in M3 (extend `LF_PoliceRanks`); checkpoint kit and search-chance tuning are M5. Skip cameras, tasers, helicopter, matrix signs, smartphone apps.
- **Lesson:** Keep the officer's discretion in choosing the charge, never the price. Fixed prices remove arguments and griefing, and they fit our crime catalog.

### Price_Guide
- **What it is:** A reference table of every shop price (seeds 200 up to a 4M truck). Shop stock is fixed-price and ignores the market. Four things sit between table and tile: reputation (20% discount at best, 80% surcharge at worst for items; 15%/60% for vehicles), cash carried (not bank), room in inventory (no space means refusal and no charge), and a free garage slot for vehicles. Petrol canister and fireworks are flat prices for everybody.
- **Our status:** Partly built. Config price lists (`LF_ShopPriceList`, `LF_StoreComponent` shelves), cash-only purchase, refusal with no charge when no room. Reputation pricing: gap and not planned; the 2026-11-08 decision explicitly avoids a reputation bar. Per-action vehicle price multipliers: #688, M3. Profit-loop check: #686.
- **Framework:** None new. #686 (no-profit-loop check) is the right guard.
- **Flagship:** Our own 1989 price list in `.conf` at fixed prices, with the same "one flat-price item everyone knows" trick (a fuel can) as a reference point.
- **Fit and priority:** Low. A hand-maintained wiki table is not a feature; generate our price page from config (website tooling) instead. Reputation-scaled prices not a fit.
- **Lesson:** Refuse the sale without taking money when the item cannot fit; publish one anchor price players can quote.

### Shops
- **What it is:** NPC counters with an action named after the shop. Pay cash only, ATM is usually on the same NPC. Traders that only buy have a "sell all" button. Clothing stores have a try-on window and an outfit basket with "buy all and wear". Hidden shops with no map marker, gang-hideout shops gated by territory points, shops that disappear during combat events. Eleven clothing shops with no overlap in stock.
- **Our status:** Built in a different shape. `LF_ShopComponent`, physical store with goods on the counter and a till (#202, closed), ATM at the counter edge. Menu-based shopping is explicitly not our direction. Dynamic sell price: decided 2026-11-07, I found no open issue (check). Try-on window: gap; era clothing is #660.
- **Framework:** Hidden-vendor and gated-vendor flags (a vendor action that needs organization ownership or a prior job count). We already have that gating for the fence.
- **Flagship:** A few distinct village shops with deliberately different stock; one back-room vendor discovered by word of mouth.
- **Fit and priority:** Low to medium. The thing to copy is "shops differ and some are hidden", which is content, M5. The outfit basket and sell-all UI do not fit "place is the interface".
- **Lesson:** Stock differences and hidden vendors make exploration pay. Do not give every shop the same list.

### Meth_cooking
- **What it is:** Heaviest illegal trade. Pump methylamine at a public depot (two pumps, 40-canister tanks that anyone can raid), then cook it in a laboratory van (1.05M or 2.9M; one canister every 3.5 to 4 s, about 4 to 6.5 min for a full load) parked at one of three marked spots, or in a house lab (gang house tier 3, 3M). The house lab has a slow self-cooking mode and a hands-on minigame: hold a drifting needle in a moving green band while hitting a marked chemical within 3 s; too many mistakes means a hidden countdown and a real explosion that burns for 10 min. Sold to one of five dealer spots, only one open at a time, rotating every 3.5 h; each sale risks landing on an informant list that police can read, wiped by a 50k bribe. Smoke and noise advertise the cook.
- **Our status:** Built in our own form. Illicit chains (#416 catalog, #417 hands-on stations: still, press, cooker; heat as smoke and smell; smell becomes a police service call). `docs/roadmap/scopes/crime/drug-chains.md` covers this. Hard drugs are "not a fit" in m5-outline. Moonshine chain exists in the town.
- **Framework:** Already the chain catalog. Missing pieces worth adding as config on existing chains: a fail-state with a hidden strike counter and a delayed blast; a rotating buyer; an informant list written on sale and read by police.
- **Flagship:** 1989 equivalent: moonshine/spirits or a home still, a travelling buyer who changes place on a timer, police questioning a regular. No meth.
- **Fit and priority:** Medium. The hidden-failure countdown and rotating buyer are cheap, produce drama, and slot into #417 stations. M5, same track as chains.
- **Lesson:** The minigame forgives errors without saying how many remain, so risk feels real. Cooking is also an announcement (smoke, noise, fixed map spots), which is what hands police the other half of the game.

### Racing
- **What it is:** Four tracks with an NPC at each start line. Prize race: same entry fee from carried cash, 2 to 10 drivers, sign-up window 5 to 10 min, whole server told; fees pool; fewer than four finishers means winner takes all, otherwise 50/30/20. Random grid slots, arrows turn green when in place, red-light start, a false start resets everyone, checkpoints visible only to you and in order, 2 to 3 min after the first finisher, refund if nobody finishes. Free time trial, same car start to finish, global leaderboard stored in a board beside the track (top 20 with vehicle name and gap). Police on duty cannot race.
- **Our status:** Gap. No race feature in `features.md`, nothing in the roadmap or issues. Closest: M5 ideas epic #214 does not list it.
- **Framework:** "Circuit race": checkpoint chain per player, grid check, entry pot with payout split config, per-track best-time record persisted per server. Fee paid in cash handed to the organiser NPC.
- **Flagship:** One or two roads and a dirt loop on Everon, pot in cash, a record plaque in a bar or garage. Period cars only.
- **Fit and priority:** Medium. Cash pot and a record board fit physical money and place-as-interface, and it gives non-criminals a hobby. Needs a persisted record schema, so decide the record format before M3 closes if it is wanted; build M5; or park behind core crime loops.
- **Lesson:** Make the loser's disconnect cost the fee, and give each racer a private next-gate so nobody can cut or sabotage another. A "no wallet, no ATM trip" rule would cut friction: put the ATM beside the NPC.

### Helicopter
- **What it is:** No shop sells one: a civilian crafts a legendary blueprint plus a rare rotor plus thousands of processed goods at crafting level 11/12, receives a vehicle document, redeems it into a garage and picks it up from the single airfield garage (75k retrieval, half refunded on storing). Two models (10 or 17 seats, 80 kg hold). Pilot licence exam 250k. Burns fuel the whole time the rotors turn; extra drain if left running in a safe zone. Inspection every 2000 km. Police have a camera helicopter with a remote fuel-drain device. Prison-break targets cannot board one.
- **Our status:** Not a fit. m5-outline lists "air and boat dealers" as not a fit and the rules ban aircraft packs. Blueprint/merge crafting is not in our tree.
- **Framework:** none (a "vehicle document" item that stays an item until redeemed at a garage is the one reusable idea, tied to garage #101).
- **Flagship:** none (a helicopter is not a 1989 village life item).
- **Fit and priority:** Not a fit. Park.
- **Lesson:** An item that stands for a high-value vehicle and can be sold between players creates a trade, but only if the redemption point is one scarce place. Also: make the grind goal visible with an itemised shopping list.

### Dynamic_market
- **What it is:** 17 goods (13 legal, 4 illegal) have prices that fall as players sell and rise as related goods are sold (families: a big eight, two pairs, one standalone). Each good has a floor and ceiling; nothing pulls prices back to default except one good, petrol, which gas stations buy by themselves. Base price is global across servers. A random single market event boosts a few goods (+10% to +100%, 60 to 120 min), announced on a broadcast, with a flame icon on boosted items. Reputation can halve payouts. Fish and apples are fixed. Beehives produce whichever honey pays more. Market is read on a phone app, a price board at the market, or the trader.
- **Our status:** Planned in principle: decisions.md 2026-11-07 "NPC prices move with supply and demand" (curves and recovery in config, fixed price = curve of 0, "scheduled after M1"). I found no open issue and no code in features.md (check). #686 profit-loop check belongs with it.
- **Framework:** Per-good price curve: base, floor, ceiling, drop per unit sold, optional recovery, optional "family" link that lifts related goods, one random event slot with multiplier and duration, and optional "world buyer" that restores price (the fuel station). All config. Persist current price and running event.
- **Flagship:** Village trader with a chalkboard price board (physical sign, updated by server) and a "price of the week" announcement posted at the market; goods tied to existing chains (planks, ore, spirits).
- **Fit and priority:** High. It is already decided and gives purpose to every gathering chain, and the persisted price table shapes the schema, so M3 (schema for per-server prices and event state), with the curve content in M5.
- **Lesson:** Price falls only when selling, so buying and shop stock stay predictable. A floor and ceiling plus family links mean a crashed good tells players what to farm next. Do not let prices recover on their own except through a visible world buyer.

### Vehicle_tuning
- **What it is:** Three public workshops plus a police one. Eleven upgrades: chip tuning (4 stages, +10% each, last two "illegal" and 25%/150% more fuel), fuel injection (3 stages, -25% each), auto-lock, airbag, false bottom (-25% chance of a police find), GPS tracker. Owner only; paid from bank; stages need earlier stages; removal costs 10% and must unwind top-down. Inspection every 500 km; overdue gives power loss and fuel use in four steps (none, -8%/+15%, -15%/+30%, -25%/+50%); HUD icon; police can check mileage.
- **Our status:** Gap. Nothing on vehicle upgrades or inspection in `features.md`. Garage, key, lock and plate are built (`LF_VehicleLockComponent`, `LF_LicensePlateManager`, `LF_Garage`). Vehicle price multipliers: #688.
- **Framework:** Per-vehicle upgrade list persisted with the vehicle record, each upgrade a config row (id, price, requires, effect keys). Optional "service due after N km" with stepwise penalty and a police read action. Hidden-compartment upgrade hooks into the search chance in contraband.
- **Flagship:** A village garage with a mechanic: engine tune-up, a hidden compartment under a seat, a lock fitted. Inspection becomes an "MOT sticker" the officer reads on the windscreen. No GPS tracker, no airbag.
- **Fit and priority:** Medium. The inspection sticker and hidden compartment create police-versus-crime play with no phone; both need new vehicle record fields, so settle the field list in M3 and build in M5. Chip tuning is a content choice, lower priority.
- **Lesson:** Make a penalty escalate in steps with a visible warning (HUD icon, hint when parking). Tie an "illegal" upgrade to a cost and a legal risk, not only a price.

### Whitelist
- **What it is:** One of three servers is whitelist-only. A player applies in Discord via a bot with a checklist (verified Discord, no mute, 50 h playtime, reputation not deep negative, plausible first and last name 3 to 18 letters, clean record). A 150+ word first-person written exam, AI text not accepted, then trial of 1 to 28 days, then permanent after review within two weeks. Rejections have cooldown tiers (4 weeks for a rule violation, 1 to 2 weeks for short or out-of-character text). A whitelisted-only dialog with 2 min auto-disconnect. Money, skills, gang and vehicles follow the account across servers; houses stay.
- **Our status:** Built core, plan around it. `LF_WhitelistManager` (file and in-game approval, police gate, decisions 2026-11-07), M4 invite-only alpha (#555), police applications reviewed by staff (#314) and form at desk (#128). A written character exam and trial period: gap. Cross-server shared account: not a fit (no external DB, M3 non-goal).
- **Framework:** Whitelist with expiry date per entry and a "trial until" state; a join gate message with reason. Probably config only.
- **Flagship:** In M4: a trial window for police and a rule gate; a short character-background question at the registration form (150 words is too heavy for our size).
- **Fit and priority:** Low to medium. Trial-until expiry on a whitelist entry is cheap and fits police applications (M4). Discord bot checklist mirrors #696 relay work; a roleplay exam and playtime threshold need #692 playtime per account (M3) first.
- **Lesson:** Make the checklist answer instantly so applicants know what to fix, and give a trial before permanence. Do not use hours played as the only gate; they also read the character.

### Community_Events
- **What it is:** Weekly staff-run one-offs (treasure hunts with clues every 10 min, team deathmatch, king of the hill, convoys), on a separate event server. Prize is event points, a separate currency (e.g. 1500/1000/500 for top three, split among team), credited by hand, spent at a cosmetic-only event shop (150 to 2500 points). Event broadcast reaches players without a phone. A summer calendar with 31 doors, seasonal decorations. Staff recruit from event helpers.
- **Our status:** Parked. decisions.md 2026-11-08 "Scheduled events are parked" and m4-outline "Not in M4: scheduled and organised events". Game Master is the likely base. Incident log and GM views are built (#239, #240).
- **Framework:** An "event currency and cosmetic shop" is the only generic piece, and it conflicts with money-is-physical; skip. A GM-triggered broadcast message is cheap.
- **Flagship:** Later: a village fair or a stolen-goods hunt run by a GM.
- **Fit and priority:** Low. Parked; revisit after M5 playtests. Non-cash prize currency is a not a fit.
- **Lesson:** Keep staff events outside the persistent economy (prizes are cosmetics, a separate server) so they cannot warp it.

### Rules
- **What it is:** Short server rules: no forced actions to save your life (no forcing bank withdrawal, garage or code handover), value of life, in-character language only, RDM only with provocation or an extended roleplay thread, no VDM, 15-minute new-life rule, no outside coordination tools, all violent participants must be in one gang, no disconnect to avoid a scene. Special rule blocks per event (gang spot, gang conflict, airdrop, evidence-locker raid, weapon transport): marked zone only, kill on sight inside, one life per event, stop when over, talk before shooting in a locker raid, safe zones override. Reporting via Discord tickets with clips; Xbox clip guide.
- **Our status:** Partly built. New Life Rule wristband (#264), lawless zone (#243, one marked area), `LF_Zones`, incident log and `/report` flow (#239, #241), server rules on the board and Discord (#312, M4). Gang-membership requirement for violence: gap. Disconnect rules: #691.
- **Framework:** Zone-scoped rule sets (the zone object says whether kill-on-sight holds, whether new-life applies, whether it counts for reputation/wanted). `LF_Zones` already has lawless; add a per-zone "event" kind.
- **Flagship:** Short 1989 rules board text at the town hall; a rule that a raid on the police store needs prior talk. Keep the blocks tiny.
- **Fit and priority:** Medium. A zone-scoped rule object is cheap and the rules copy is M4 (#312). Do the zone flag in M3 if config schema covers it.
- **Lesson:** State the rule exceptions inside each event, and let a physical zone (not a timer) decide when the rules change. A "you may not be forced to hand over bank, vehicles or code" line is worth copying.

### Evidence_locker
- **What it is:** A fortified compound that stores everything police confiscate (cash never goes in; police gear, press kit and bags that still hold items are refused). A gang can scout it for a rough fill level in four words (empty, little, well stocked, packed), then raid it with at least 5 members online, with police on duty. Five barriers in order: cutting torch on the outer shell, lockpicks on grille doors, a fuse box puzzle, lockpicks again with power cut, then a thermal drill (about 22 min; any officer can abort and destroy it; no replacement within 10 min ends the raid). 75 min to open, 30 min to loot, loot is lost on disconnect nearby. Raid ends if no civilian on the site for 5 min. Cooldown 6 h after any raid. A full locker triggers a police weapon transport event.
- **Our status:** Built in part. Evidence locker (#269, persisted `evidencelocker`, bags logged against crime reports), forced-open locker via `LF_Breach`, fence tools (#271, #273 drill with jam chance), breach framework, fence-tool serials leave traces. A confiscation vault that fills and drains into a loot raid with staged barriers: gap.
- **Framework:** "Staged vault" chain: ordered barriers each needing a named tool, one barrier needing a second worker at a different place (fuse box), a defender action that resets a barrier, cooldown after a raid, and a fill-level hint as a scouting action. Many pieces already exist in `LF_Breach` and `LF_BankVaultComponent`.
- **Flagship:** The police-store armoury or village evidence cage; drill and charge from the fence; a defender can cut power or pull the drill; loot is the stuff police really seized.
- **Fit and priority:** High for the two-worker barrier, defender reset and fill-level scouting: they add multi-worker hands-on play on code we already have (extends #275 armory heist, M5 multi-stage heist #662/#663). Cooldown and fill-level need persistence, so M3 schema check, build M5.
- **Lesson:** Confiscated goods feeding a robbable store links police work to crime drama. Give defenders a direct counter (abort the drill) so the heist needs a guard, and never show exact stock.

### Server_concept
- **What it is:** Pitch page: 128 players on one island, no campaign, almost everything done by players. Player-made economy (ore, ingots, a market that moves with sales, auction house), one civilian life with many jobs, crime with two contested gang spots, police as players ("every heist is somebody else's emergency call"), 12 skills and 11 professions levelled by doing, account-wide money and skills across servers, weekly events, plenty of non-violent play (fishing, chess, arcade, a newspaper, races). Onboarding quest chain.
- **Our status:** Overlaps our design (`docs/design.md`): player-made economy, player police, skills by use (`LF_Progression`), onboarding via quests (`LF_QuestTrackerComponent`). Cross-server account: not a fit. Auction house and company registration: not in our tree (M6 or parked, check).
- **Framework:** none new.
- **Flagship:** Our own 1989 pitch already exists; add a "not all crime" paragraph with fishing and a bar.
- **Fit and priority:** Low (positioning only). M2.5 landing page copy could borrow the structure.
- **Lesson:** Say plainly that most players never touch crime, then show crime as the thing that creates others' emergencies.

### Supremacy_System
- **What it is:** After a gang takes one of two spots, each held hour is +1 point for the holder and -1 for every other gang with points, capped at 50; zero wipes the account. Benefits split into ownership-only (gang bank access, store vehicles, one spot has a smuggler chest and vacuum sealer, the other has weapon repair at -25%) and point-gated (10 points opens a shop, 20 a garage, 30 a spawn point). Fights: held zone scoring every second, inner zone counts double, capped per side, takes about 20 min; 30 min lockout after capture, 10 min after a real defence. Points are per server. A gang menu shows what is still missing.
- **Our status:** Partly built. `LF_Hideout` and organizations (shared funds, upgrades) exist, with an ownership-only model; no contested capture, supremacy decay or point-gated tiers (check in `docs/roadmap/scopes/ownership/hideouts.md`). Nothing planned in M3-M5 for gang territory; m4 says no scheduled events.
- **Framework:** "Held-ground rent": a place records hold-hours per organization, one point per hour, decay for rivals, cap, with config thresholds that unlock named perks (shop, garage, spawn). Contest scoring by presence in a ring.
- **Flagship:** Two or three physical places (the quarry yard, the old mill) where an organization's hold unlocks a back-room vendor or a still. Overlaps the lawless quarry (#243).
- **Fit and priority:** Medium. It turns hideouts into long-term player drama (a week of holding versus an evening) and the points table is per-server persisted data, so M3 for schema and M5 for content; or M6 together with land. Skip kill-count scoring that rewards zerg.
- **Lesson:** Points decay with rent so a one-night capture is worth nothing and holding is the game; show a progress sheet that lists each unlock and how many points are missing.

### Press
- **What it is:** Player-run newspaper in a Discord channel, run by a small editorial team (publisher, editors, columnists, freelancers, economic analyst, police liaison). Reporters get a blue vest and a handheld camera, banned from using them as cover during crime. Ad space sold for in-game money. Join by interview after 50 hours and a 2-week trial with 2 published articles.
- **Our status:** Gap. No newspaper or reporter in `features.md`, roadmap or issues. Our Discord and website exist as community channels.
- **Framework:** none beyond a "press" role kit (vest and camera item) with a rule flag; the articles live outside the game.
- **Flagship:** A weekly "village gazette" posted on a physical notice board that the server rebuilds from the incident log (arrests, robberies, price of the week). Possible M4 community or M5 flavour.
- **Fit and priority:** Low. It is volunteer labour and external channel; a gazette board generated from the event bus is cheap and fits "player drama is the hook".
- **Lesson:** A press role works because it has a no-cover rule. Players can feel the world's stories without more systems if the server prints them.

### Discord_verification
- **What it is:** Link an in-game account to a Discord account by pasting a personal token from the settings menu into a Discord bot. Verified perks: faster support, a requirement for police applications and events, a badge in the player menu, a civilian bonus paycheck of 2000 every 5 min while playing, stopping once the player is wealthy.
- **Our status:** Not built; related #696 Discord welcome relay, M4 staff and moderation. Our account uid is the key for whitelists and bans (decisions 2026-11-09).
- **Framework:** Optional "link token" for account-to-Discord identity in the staff terminal (M5 #654/#655).
- **Flagship:** None in-game. A link only helps staff find a report.
- **Fit and priority:** Low (M4 live ops). The bonus paycheck is a poor idea (a payment from outside the world; we already have unemployment benefit, `LF_UnemploymentBenefit`, which is wealth-gated like theirs).
- **Lesson:** A wealth cap turns a free income into a leg-up for newer players. Do not hide a stat that matters behind an off-game link.

### Cross-cutting patterns (group 0)

- **Fixed rules, player decisions.** Fines are fixed by a law table, ranks are fixed, market curves are formulas: the server never improvises, so the human choice (which charge, what to farm, when to cook) is the whole game. Our crime catalog (#685) and price curve decision follow this.
- **Every crime is an announcement.** Smoke and noise on a cook, a map marker for each robbery, dispatch for a tampered camera: the world tells police where to go, so crime is a story for someone else. This is the same hook as ours, and our heat/smell service calls already do it.
- **Hands-on stations with a visible cost of failure.** The house lab, the vault drill and racing grid all demand a person on a spot, with a mistake budget the player cannot see, and a defender who can break it. Good fit for our multi-worker stations; avoid timer-only steps.
- **Scarce, public, decaying rewards.** Supremacy is rent (decay per hour), the open drug dealer rotates every 3.5 h, market floors and ceilings cap farming. A reward that has to be maintained or chased keeps the loop alive better than a fixed unlock.
- **Cash carried, one conversion point.** Shops, racing fees and fines draw from pocket cash and put an ATM at the same NPC; tickets come out of cash and a full pay-off comes from the bank. This matches our "ATM is the only boundary" rule; the lesson is placing the ATM beside every cash sink.
- **Features we should not copy.** The wiki's depth rests on a smartphone (wanted list, dispatch, market, car finder, radio apps), helicopters, tasers, speed cameras, event points and cross-server accounts. In 1989 each becomes a physical board, a paper form, a radio call or is dropped.

### Vehicle_Guide
- **What it is:** A fleet comparison for about 20 civilian vehicles (10.000$ jeep to 4.000.000$ canopy truck). The page's thesis: cargo weight capacity (40 kg jeep to 350 kg Ural) decides income, and a boot also has a per-item size limit and special-cargo rules (fuel and chemicals only in matching tanker/lab vehicles, parcel cap per vehicle). Vehicles bind to the owner; a 4+ digit code shares access and changing it wipes the list. Only Urals and one lab van can be lockpicked (boot only, 5 min open to everyone, 30 min cooldown, whole server is told). Also: 500 km inspections, abandoned cars towed back after ~45 min, garage buy-back at a third, reputation moving dealer prices -15%/+60%, chip tuning with diminishing returns, hold-to-flip/repair, cruise control.
- **Our status:** Partly built. Vehicle lot sells vanilla S105/UAZ/S1203/UAZ452/M923, keys and `LF_VehicleLockComponent`, plates, garage store/retrieve plus impound ("garage it to keep it"), lockpick a car and hotwire (#412, built). Planned: per-action price multipliers (#688, M3), world cleanup of abandoned vehicles (#689/#690 area, M3), repair kit and unflip (#648/#649, M5). Decided against: heavy upkeep (m5-outline: fuel and simple repairs only, no parts). Gap: cargo-weight differentiation as a deliberate stat, shared-code access, cruise control. Reputation pricing: we have a hidden crime record, no reputation bar (decision 2026-11-08), so not a fit as shown.
- **Framework:** Per-vehicle cargo budget and per-item size/type rules as config on the vehicle catalogue (we mostly inherit vanilla storage; expose it in the `vehicles` config section). A "special cargo" tag so a tanker-only item is refused elsewhere. A price modifier hook (already #688).

### Cross-cutting patterns (group 1)

- **Gates tell you what is missing.** Level, profession, licence and reputation gates all refuse in plain words naming the missing requirement. Our chain gate already does this; copy the habit across new systems.
- **Money sinks are flat and repeatable.** Rent once, upgrade in same-price steps, repair a limited number of times, pay the instructor per attempt. Nothing is a percentage fee that grows, which keeps tuning easy.
- **State that matters is persisted, and refunds follow server faults.** Plants, weapon wear, machine contents and an event in progress all survive a restart; an exam cut by a restart refunds. Settle these record shapes in M3, not after.
- **Risk is placed in the world, not in a menu.** Unprotected plants, public machine boxes, lockpickable trunks, loot only saved on exit from the zone: every pattern lets another player be the threat, which is our "player drama is the hook".
- **The costly ones are social structures.** Gang ranks, conflicts, hideout captures and police applications are the pieces that generate stories. Item stat tables (weapons, vehicles, fish) mostly provide the economy around them.
- **Things we should not copy:** phone apps for everything (SmartGrow, Wallet, Black Market), reputation as a price modifier, passive machines that fill themselves, XP popups, and any cross-server account state.

### Drug_dealer_profession
- **What it is:** A civilian role that costs the most of any profession (about 2.5 times the standard), has no skill gate, and locks you out of packing, pumping and cooking. Each sale drains the zone's bonus (towards half its base) and it recovers when left alone, so dealers rotate. Boards show the top 9 dealers' average share and volume over three days. Zones push the dealer's list to producers while a dealer stands in one. Heavy volume sends an anonymous tip to police.

### Cross-cutting patterns (group 2)

- **Hauling is the progression.** Housing, crafting inputs and the transport event all turn a goal into physical carrying and station use, with no timers. This matches our principle; the price is heavy item volumes, so tune carry weight and vehicle load first.
- **Intermediaries with a diminishing bonus.** The dealing-zone bonus and the dynamic market both pay less the more you use one spot or good, which pushes movement and variety. Our config-driven price curve and a per-zone bonus use the same mechanism.
- **Gates by profession plus level, one unlock each.** Each job opens one station or tool at a level rather than a perk tree. Cheap to build, easy to read.
- **Event shells with asymmetric roles.** Prison break and weapon transport share a pattern: preconditions from world state, prep time, a hot phase with the safe zone lifted, a cooldown, and a reward only for the hunters. Build one shell and reuse it.
- **Money flows are all prepaid, bank-based and capped.** Tax is prepaid in blocks, auction fees are non-refundable, company fees scale with members. Our physical-cash rule means these need counters, paper receipts and cash handover; design the sink first, then the counter.
- **Public information becomes a game.** Boards of dealer shares, price apps and the informant list make hidden info a social lever. In 1989 the equivalent is a notice board or a chalk price at the market; do not build leaderboards as phone apps.

### Cross-cutting patterns (group 3)

- **Penalties are legible, losses are ladders.** Every loss has a tier (self-store safe, tow loses cargo, impound costs double) and a message that names the cost. Our garage and death rules can copy the tiers, not the numbers.
- **Free-fire contexts instead of PvP bans.** Reputation, conflicts, airdrops and events all define when killing is free, announced to everyone, and everywhere else costs. This matches our murder-wanted and zone model and gives us a place to attach a "declared feud" record.
- **Money sinks are layered and explicit:** retrieval fees, slot prices climbing 20%, repair share of price with a repair cap, 5% auction listing fee, bank wire fee. We have fixed-fee sinks; a growth curve per slot is cheap to add.
- **Gating by profession plus skill, not by money alone.** Convenience tools and machines need both; the NPC says which half is missing. Our job levels already fit this and want the same refusal copy.
- **Server-wide state signals make crime social:** alarms, map markers and dispatch (gas station) and announcements (conflicts) create player drama without scripted content. The place-is-the-interface rule says keep them in world (alarm, blip on the dispatch board), never as a phone broadcast.
- **Much of ELAN is account-wide or phone-based:** a shared account across servers, streaks, achievements, Elo ladders. All conflict with our per-server persistence and 1989 period, so skip them; the transferable ideas are the rule shapes, not the surfaces.
