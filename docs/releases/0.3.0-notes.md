# Life Framework 0.3.0: player release notes (draft)

Status: draft, not posted. Written on 2026-11-26 from what is merged on `main` and was seen working in the
agent playtest rounds of 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-27. The release candidate can change it, so recheck every list
the day it goes out. Nothing here promises a date or a future feature. The format follows the 0.2.0 changelog
in [the Workshop changelog](../marketing/workshop/changelog.md): short lines, plain words.

## Full notes

### New jobs and loops

- **Iron, start to finish.** Mine iron at Highstone hill, dig coal at the East Rise seam, and smelt both into
  an iron bar at the furnace on the east compound. The furnace takes two iron nuggets and one coal and runs
  for a minute. It pays smelting experience, and a refusal tells you which trade and level you need.
- **Coal.** The East Rise seam opens at Mining 4. Sell coal lumps at the power plant's coal buyer.
- **Lumber.** Fell trees, saw the logs, and sell planks and boards at the timber yard. Planks now carry as
  bundles of four.
- **Tools that wear.** Pickaxes, axes and rods come in three tiers (Worn, Standard, Fine). They wear down with
  use, and the hardware store's bench repairs and sharpens them for a fee.
- **Levels and perks.** Every trade levels with a sound and a card that names what the level opens. At
  level 10 you pick a perk at the labor office (double yield, faster work or less tool wear). Your first
  shift of the day pays double experience for 30 minutes. A shift board at the mine and the sawmill ranks
  the workers of the week.
- **Truck license and hauling.** The truck yard on the north-east compound sells trucks to people who hold a
  truck license. The license costs $300: an examiner lends you a truck and you drive four gates in order. A
  wrong turn fails the course, and you wait before a retake. A cargo truck bed takes a full load of logs, and a
  full bed refuses the next one and says why. A truck you left on the street across a restart goes to the
  impound, and you collect it there for a fee.
- **Export board.** At Hauling 10 the board at the dock warehouse offers export orders (iron bars, copper bars,
  coal or planks for the ships). Take one, bring the goods to the crane spot, and the hand-in pays. A short
  load pays nothing and is refused. Every order fits in a cargo truck.
- **Medic calls.** Clock in at the clinic and casualties appear around the island (the mine, woodlot, farm,
  dock, roadside). The call shows on the board and pager. Take it, draw an ambulance from the car pool, treat
  the patient, or carry them to a clinic bed. Treating and delivering pay bonuses at clock-out, up to an
  hourly cap.
- **Police booking desk.** Cuff someone and book them at the Saint-Philippe station. The officer ticks the
  charges on a charge sheet and signs. The suspect then chooses to pay the fine at once or take the time.
  Crimes that carry jail only offer the cell. Jail sentences now top out at 15 minutes. Your F2 "My file" tab
  lists your tickets, bookings and any license that was taken.
- **Run a plate.** An officer can run the plate of the car in front and read the answer on the pager.
- **Gang registry.** Found a gang at the registry in town ($250), pick a flag from the flag wall, and invite
  people by scroll. Buy a gang radio ($60) at the gun counter and tune it, and your gang shares a channel.
- **North outpost.** A rebel camp with gate guards, a quartermaster and a chalk board. It sells a gang radio
  to anyone and a pistol once the camp knows you. A rifle needs more standing than that.
- **Bars and rumors.** Ask the barkeepers at the Chotain and Levie bars and the shopkeepers "Heard anything?"
  to hear a rumor, one a day.

### The town

- **Shops with goods on the shelves.** The pharmacy, hardware store, general store, gun store, butcher, bakery
  and village bar show single items on their shelves instead of empty boards. Shop doors carry opening-hours
  plates.
- **Night lighting.** At 22:00 the street lamps, shop fronts and the insides of the hostel, pub, fuel station,
  gun counter, newsagent and registry light up. Dock lamps light the export board and the crane spot.
- **Signs.** New signs mark the respray bay, the seed and feed barn, the labor office, the shift boards, the
  bus timetable and the road junctions on the way into town. Signs use US English.
- **A respray bay.** Park your own car on the pad at the garage and pick a paint job at the bench for $150.
- **Voices.** The clerk, registrar, barkeepers, outpost people and the people on the square speak recorded lines.
- **Sounds of the day.** The church bell rings at 07:00, the ship's horn sounds at 19:00 and a whistle marks
  the start of the shift at the mine and the sawmill.
- **The newsagent** sells the *Everon Courier*, a road map and a tourist map.

### Balance

- **Pay order.** Police duty is now the lowest-paid steady work. Medics earn a little more. Civilian trades at the
  high tiers pay more than both, and the trades climb with level and tier.
- **Duty bonuses.** Police and medics earn small bonuses for outcomes (a call closed, a patient treated or
  delivered). Bonuses are capped per hour.
- **Hauling pays by truck.** A truck load of boards pays well while the timber buyer still wants boards. What
  the buyer turns down goes to the wholesale counter.

### Fixes

- A desk job (miner, lumberjack, farmer) is kept after a restart and after you respawn.
- Paying out your last cash no longer freezes the screen.
- Ambulances can be drawn from the car pool again without an error.
- A tool drawn in your hand counts as held, so the felling prompt shows with the axe drawn.
- Messages no longer show a bracketed debug title such as "[INFO]".
- Level refusals name the trade and the level you need.
- Store shelf goods stay on the shelves when the town sweeps up loose items.
- The welcome guide no longer opens in the middle of a conversation.
- The lot's lock row shows the full plate. The charge sheet shows each fine and says when it is capped. The
  cell gate says the cell is locked.
- Many props that floated or sat in the grass were moved onto level ground. Signs that were cut by wall trim
  were moved off the wall.

### Known issues

- **Cars after a restart.** Cars parked on the street can come back twice after a restart, and the extra copy
  can be unlocked.
- **Robbery kit.** The vault drill may not pick up or work in the robbery kit near the bank. We could not
  repeat this on a server, and it is still open.
- **Bank vault on a dedicated server.** Vault offers and the fuse step are not fully proven for players who join
  a dedicated server.
- **Police menu.** The menu at the duty NPC has two blank buttons that do nothing.
- **Shop price list.** The Receipt and Leave buttons can sit below the edge of the sheet.
- **Plates.** License plate text may not show on a car, and a late joiner may not see it.
- **Controls.** The debug menu can open on a gamepad shoulder tap, and F9 and U clash with the game's own keys.
- **Ambulance bay.** The first ambulance draw can be refused with "a vehicle stands in the bay" when nothing is
  there. Try again a few minutes later.
- **Chess board.** The last column of keys is cut off.
- **Sounds and voices.** The test rounds checked that bells, whistles and voices start, but nobody judged them
  by ear. Expect uneven volume or timing.
- **The Courier.** The paper is on sale, but a fresh world has no weekly edition to read.
- **Supermarket shelves.** They can look empty in a new world until the store restocks.
- **Night at the rebel camp.** The camp's lamps and fire were added after the last night test and were not
  re-checked.
- **Not tested.** Farming and fishing on the island were not re-tested in the last round. The sale at the
  wholesale counter once the premium buyer has enough, a bench repair with a bought pickaxe, gang member marks on
  the map and the gang bars were not replayed in the final rounds. Nothing was tested with a human at the keys
  for a full ten hours.
- **A save may be wiped.** This is still an alpha server. We announce a wipe 24 hours before it happens.
