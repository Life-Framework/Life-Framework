# Economy review, 2026-11-17

An adversarial review of the whole money and economy area, not just recent diffs: `Feature/Money`, `ATM` (banking), `Account`, `Shop` (shops, stores, stocks, fence), `Trader`, `Catalogue`, `Processing`, `Gathering`, `Chains`, `Jobs` pay, and the price configs in both addons. The hunt focused on duplication, free money, and prices or amounts trusted from the client. Two passes ran (the `interrogate` skill):

- an Opus reviewer covered gathering, processing, chains, jobs pay, the trader and the account files;
- a second pass covered money, the ATM, shops and stores, the catalogue, persistence of bank and ledger, the debug top-up and starting money. It also ran a cross-config price scan: every prefab's highest sell price against its lowest buy price across the catalogue, `ShopPrices` and the shop stocks.

**Result:** 5 confirmed (1 fixed here, 4 filed as #2407, #2408 and #2409), 1 plausible, 17 dismissed.

The core money paths hold up:
- every server RPC re-derives the price and the amount;
- cash moves all-or-nothing with rollback;
- the ATM is range-checked;
- the bank and the carried-cash snapshot save in the same store write.

The holes are in the pay-outs around the edges.

## Confirmed

### 1. The unemployment benefit ignores carried cash (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Jobs/LF_UnemploymentBenefit.c` `CollectAtCounter`. The means test read only the ATM bank balance against `m_iBenefitBalanceThreshold` (500).

**Scenario:** a player of any wealth keeps their money as cash and never deposits. Their bank stays at 0, so they collect $100 every 20 minutes, forever. Both passes found it independently.

**Fix:** the means test is now the bank balance plus `LF_MoneyUtils.GetCash(user)`. `CollectAtCounter` resolves the uid and calls the new seam `CollectFor(uid, user)`. The refusal string now reads "You have too much money for the benefit".

**Test:** `LF_Test_BenefitCountsCash` (`jobs/benefit-counts-cash`, WORLD). Two bodies, both with an empty bank: one carries 5000 cash and is refused with its cash untouched; the broke one still collects.
- Red on the unfixed code: `carried cash above the threshold collects nothing (expected 0, got 100)` and `the refused claim left the cash as it was (expected 5000, got 5100)`.
- Green with the fix, alongside `jobs/unemployment-benefit` and the progression persist test.

### 2. The trader sale runs on the client: every sale fails on a dedicated server (#2407)

**Where:** `Components/InventorySystem/LF_InventoryStorageManagerComponent.c:3`. The sale overrides `SCR_InventoryStorageManagerComponent.InsertItem`, which vanilla calls from the inventory UI on the client: it checks `IsAnimationReady()` and plays UI sounds. Off the server, `AddCash` returns 0, so `FailSell` runs on every sale.

The suite misses it because `LF_Test_E2E_Narrative` calls `InsertItem` on the server. The trader is placed only in DebugWorld.

**Outcome:** a server RPC with ownership, range and price checks is a feature-sized change. Filed #2407.

### 3. Abandoning a delivery run keeps the cargo (#2408)

**Where:** `Feature/Jobs/LF_JobAction.c:321` and `LF_CivilianJob.AbandonRun`. Abandon ends the run without `TakeCargo`, and nothing stops the next start.

**Scenario:** start, abandon, repeat. Each loop mints a full vanilla jerrycan (fuel delivery) or a parcel.

**Outcome:** taking the cargo back alone is dodged by stashing it in a car first, so the rule needs a decision (a cooldown, a charge, or run-tagged cargo). Filed #2408.

### 4. A delivery or taxi run pays with no distance, time or passenger check (#2408)

**Where:** `LF_CivilianJob.CompleteRun` and `LF_JobDropoffAction`.
- A taxi run has no cargo, so `TakeCargo` returns true and the drop-off pays 80.
- A courier run pays at any drop-off, however close it is to the start.

**Scenario:** in GridWorld the taxi pad is about 6 m from a drop-off. Start then drop off pays 80 in seconds, repeatable. The flagship town has no job counters yet.

**Outcome:** filed with #2408 (record the start position and time, require a minimum distance and duration, and require a passenger for a fare).

### 5. Gather nodes with no cap print money (#2409)

**Where:** `Configs/Resources/ResourceChains.conf`. The apple node has no gather cap and no cooldown, a 1 s action and no tool, and the flagship town places an apple tree.
- Apples sell for 4–5, so one tree yields roughly 250 a minute plus gather XP.
- Cement, gravel and sand are uncapped too (10 bricks are worth 120 at the trader), and so is ripe cabbage.

**Outcome:** a balance call, filed as #2409.
