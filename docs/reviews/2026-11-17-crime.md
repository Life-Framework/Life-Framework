# Crime and policing review, 2026-11-17

This is an adversarial review of the whole crime and policing area, not only recent diffs:

- `Feature/Crime`: robbery, vaults, safe types, breach, fence, heists, cash van, chop shop, contraband, lockpick, hotwire, murder.
- `Feature/Police`: duty, armory, ranks, wanted, dispatch, roadblocks, spikes, tickets, cuffs, escort, jail, disconnect rules.
- `Feature/Investigation`.
- `Feature/Hideouts`.

It used the `interrogate` skill. Four read-only reviewer passes split the area: bank and robbery, contraband and vehicles with hideouts, jail and wanted with cuffs, and duty with investigation. The lead then checked every finding marked confirmed below against the code.

**Result:** 9 confirmed. 6 are fixed in this PR, each with a test that failed on the unfixed code. The other 3 are filed. 43 are plausible: 31 are filed in four grouped issues (#2416, #2417, #2418, #2419) and 12 are left open, each with a reason. 25 are dismissed.

What a player can no longer do after this PR:

- lose a car at the chop shop for nothing;
- get wanted for a robbery that paid nothing;
- be jailed for logging out after an earlier escort;
- arrest themself or a dead body from the menu;
- get locked out of jail work by logging out mid-task.

## Confirmed

### 1. A till robbery that pays nothing still punishes (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Crime/LF_RobAction.c` `CommitRobbery`.

**Problem:** `PayCash` rolls back a short payment and returns 0. `CommitRobbery` then still started the till cooldown, raised wanted, called dispatch and opened the witness scene.

**Scenario:** a robber with full pockets robs a till. They get no cash, the police are called, they are wanted, and the till is locked for everyone for its cooldown. The robber sees no message.

**Fix:** a 0 payment returns `REFUSED_NO_ROOM` before any of that happens. The robber gets the toast `#LF-Robbery_NoRoom`.

**Test:** `crime/rob-no-room` (WORLD, a robber with no inventory). On the unfixed code it failed with "the robber is not made wanted for cash they never took (expected 0, got 1)" and "the till cooldown does not start (expected 0, got 1712.03)".

### 2. Chop-shop COLLECT deletes the car when the cash does not fit (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Crime/LF_ChopShop.c` `ServerCollect`.

**Problem:** `ServerCollect` ignored the amount `AddCash` actually paid. A full inventory takes 0, because overflow is not dropped.

**Scenario:** a worker with full pockets presses COLLECT. The car is deleted and its ownership record scrapped, a wanted report is raised, and nothing is paid.

**Fix:** COLLECT pays first. If the payment is short, it is rolled back and COLLECT refuses with `NO_ROOM` (`#LF-Chop_NoRoom`), and the car stays. The rule is `LF_ChopShopRules.CheckCollect`.

**Test:** `chopshop/collect-guard` (LOGIC). Its red proof run failed "a full inventory that took no cash keeps the car (expected 12, got 0)".

### 3. Chop-shop COLLECT deletes a car with someone inside (fixed)

**Where:** same function.

**Problem:** LOAD refuses an occupied car, but COLLECT did not check again.

**Scenario:** a keyed owner gets back into the car while it is being stripped. COLLECT deletes the car with them in it.

**Fix:** `CheckCollect` refuses with `OCCUPIED`. The pass marked this item plausible; reading the code confirms it.

**Test:** the same test, "a car with someone inside is not collected".

### 4. An escort hold outlives the arrest, so a later logout counts as escaping custody (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Police/LF_Jail.c` `Imprison`, `LF_JailEscort.c`.

**Problem:** only the vehicle `Arrive` path cleared a hold. An officer who grabbed a suspect and then used the arrest action or the menu left the hold active for good. `LF_DisconnectRules` treats an escorted player as cuffed (`m_bEscortedCountsAsCuffed`).

**Scenario:** a prisoner serves their sentence and is released. Later they log out normally. On return they are jailed again, with at least 300 s and +1 wanted, and a conviction row.

**Fix:** `Imprison`, which every arrest path goes through, calls the new `LF_JailEscort.Drop`.

**Test:** `jail/escort-drop` (WORLD). It failed on the unfixed code with "the cell ends the escort hold" and "no hold is left for the officer to drop (expected 0, got 1)".

### 5. The police-menu arrest skipped the hands-on gates (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Police/LF_PoliceMenu.c` `RpcAsk_LF_PoliceArrest`, `LF_Jail.c` `Arrest`.

**Problem:** the in-world arrest action runs `LF_PlayerInteraction.CheckPair` (self, alive, reach) and the new-character grace check. The menu RPC ran only a distance check.

**Scenario:** a client can arrest a wanted player's dead body, a new character still in its grace, or the on-duty officer themself, whose own body is always in reach.

**Fix:** the RPC now runs `CheckPair` and `LF_JailArrestAction.RefusedByGrace`, and `LF_Jail.Arrest` refuses officer == prisoner for every path.

**Test:** `jail/self-arrest` (WORLD). It failed on the unfixed code with "an officer cannot arrest themself" and "no sentence starts".

The `CheckPair` and grace lines in the RPC have no automated test, because driving the RPC needs a connected character. They reuse the action's gates unchanged.

### 6. A logout mid-task locks a prisoner out of jail work (fixed)

**Where:** `Feature/Police/LF_JailWork.c` `Finish`.

**Problem:** the timer returned on a missing worker entity before it removed the prisoner from the busy list.

**Scenario:** a prisoner starts a task and logs out. After rejoining, every task is refused as BUSY until a world reset, so they can never work time off again.

**Fix:** the uid travels with the timer and is cleared first.

**Test:** `jail/work-abandon` (WORLD). Its red proof run failed "after rejoining the prisoner may work again (expected 0, got 2)".

### 7. Armory kit farm by toggling duty (filed #2416)

`LF_PoliceRanks.OnDutyChangedAt` (`LF_PoliceRanks.c:441`) clears the one-kit-per-shift flag on every duty change, and the flag is not saved.

Scenario: an officer draws the kit (M9, M16, spike strip, roadblock kit), hands it to a friend, clocks out and in, and draws again. Repeating this arms any number of players.

A good fix needs a choice: a persisted cooldown, or a cap on outstanding serials. So it is filed rather than patched here.

### 8. The station promote action never finds an officer (filed #2416)

`LF_RankPromoteAction` (`LF_ArmoryAction.c:121`) calls `FindLowerRankNearby`, which walks `LF_PoliceRanks.s_aBoundIds`. Only tests call `BindOfficer`, so in play the list is null and every promote is refused.

Ranks still rise through duty time.

The fix is a player-manager scan, and only a two-player session can prove it. Filed.

### 9. Bagging an evidence marker pulls the item out of whoever now holds it (filed #2419)

`LF_Evidence.Bag` (`Feature/Investigation/LF_Evidence.c:469`) resolves the row's item and `LF_EvidenceBagComponent.Seal` attaches it to the bag. Nothing checks that the item is still at the scene; `Sweep` only checks that the marker exists.

Scenario: a civilian drops a weapon at a scene, a marker is tagged, and the civilian picks the weapon up again. An officer bags the marker and the weapon leaves the civilian's inventory.

The fix needs a world test with an inventory. Filed.
