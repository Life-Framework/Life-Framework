**Truck purse check.** The 11-26 round measured one full-purse hardwood cycle at $1,237/h. The timber counter's
premium row refills 28 boards each real hour (`LF_BuyerDemand.Refill` counts real seconds, not game hours), so after
the first 32 boards the rest go to the wholesale curve: $786 the first hour, $742 an hour after that. The $1,237
figure is one cycle extrapolated to an hour.

**Session spot-checks (island, full profile, 2026-11-26).** `truck-haul-proof` loaded a 12-unit bed from 3 hardwood
trees and sawed it in a 9.8-minute cycle, but its sell step failed: the harness could not pick up the sawn boards
("none in reach"). `mining-iron-chain` timed one iron rock at 78 s for 5 pieces and a 5-batch smelt at 48 s (the
model assumes 60 s a run); its later pickups hit the same harness error. Both are harness faults, not pay faults.

**Manual.** The jobs table quoted 0.2.0 duty pay (police $900 to $1,260, medic $792 to $1,224). It now matches the
duty ladder, and the fishing and lumber rows match `economy`.
