# The anniversary

*Story chapter, M8 Neighbours. The fourth arc of Everon 1989. Public pitch only.*

Three years ago the Zone was emptied and its families were sent here. On the day the town marks it, the
relocated stand on the square beside the people who took them in. Nobody agrees what the day is for: a
remembrance, a thank-you, or the town's chance to say the matter is closed.

Three people organise it, and each wants the day to say something different: the mayor, the priest and a widow
who lost her husband in the evacuation.

## What a player sees

1. **Preparations.** The week before, the square fills with trestles and bunting. The mayor needs hands to carry
   chairs and string lamps, the priest needs candles from the general store, and the widow wants the names read
   out. Each of them asks a player they trust.
2. **The memorial.** At dusk the town walks from the church to the square. The priest speaks, the mayor speaks,
   the widow lays the first candle. Where a player stands in that walk, and what they carried, is noticed.
3. **A letter from the past.** After the speeches a letter reaches the square, written three years ago and held
   back until now. Someone has to decide whether it is read aloud.
4. **The town gathers.** The crowd stays after dark. What the regulars say about the letter, and about the
   players who carried it, is the town's answer. It sets which way Everon leans from here.

## Who you meet

- **The mayor**, who has run the town hall since before the evacuation and wants the day to go smoothly.
- **The priest**, who wants it to be honest.
- **The widow**, who wants her husband's name said in public.
- **The clerk**, **Odile Marchand**, **Gaspard Lenoir** and **Rémi Duval**, the faces the player already knows.
  Each stands in the crowd with a different view of the day.

## Where it happens

The church, where the walk starts, and the square, where the memorial and the gathering take place. The general
store and the town hall are the stops for the preparations.

## Why it matters

It closes the four chapters. The shipment's choices and the town's standing carry into the square, and the
players' choice that night decides whether the relocated keep the island's goodwill. The town remembers it.
