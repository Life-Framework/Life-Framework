# The shipment

*Story chapter, M7 Power and turf. The third arc of Everon 1989. Public pitch only.*

Smuggling comes to the coast. A ship from the mainland ties up at the Saint-Philippe quay at dawn with the
week's cargo, and the papers say it is tinned fish for the harbour co-op. The harbour master signed for it.
He is not sure he should have.

Three groups want that cargo and none of them says so out loud: a gang that holds the coast road, the
gendarmes, and the town, which lives off the dock work and would rather not lose it.

## What a player sees

1. **The ship docks.** At dawn a truck backs onto the quay and the dockers walk crates into the warehouse.
   Harbour master Marcel Roux watches from the door with a clipboard.
2. **A crate is opened.** Marcel asks a player he trusts to count one crate that weighs too much. What is in it
   is theirs to deal with: hand it to the police desk, or take it to a buyer who asks no questions.
3. **Rumours in the pub.** By evening the café talks. What the regulars say depends on what the player did,
   and the talk fades after a few days.
4. **A raid.** If the police were told, they come to the warehouse. The gendarmes are at the door, and officers
   on duty get the call.

## Who you meet

- **Marcel Roux**, the harbour master. Already on the quay; this is the first time he asks for more than
  carrying crates.
- **A dock foreman** who runs the crew and keeps his eyes on the floor.
- **A go-between** for the gang on the coast road, who drinks in the village pub.
- **An officer** from the gendarmerie, who wants the cargo more than the culprit.
- The café's regulars, who say what the town is thinking.

## Where it happens

The dock warehouse, the harbour cabin, the village pub, the police desk, and wherever a buyer is standing this
week.

## Why it matters

It joins the factory story to the turf war: whoever controls the route controls the coast. Each choice moves
your standing with the town, the police or the underworld, and the town remembers it.
