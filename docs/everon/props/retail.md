## How to fit out a shop (verified facts only)

1. Sales point: CounterShop_01 (1.04 m top) with CashierShop_01 (0.51 m) and optionally ScaleShop_01 or BreadSlicer_01 on it; CounterShop and CashierShop each sit in all four towns (28 / 30 placed), so vanilla shops already pair them.
2. Stock: ShelfShop_01 (2.48 m, five boards 0.10-1.43 m) and ShelfUniversal_01 wide/narrow/corner (six boards to 2.59 m) are the aisle units; ShelfShop_02 (0.2 m deep) is the back-of-counter shelf. Boards are real horizontal collider tops, so a prop can be placed on them by height.
3. Cold goods: CoolingBoxShop_01 (open chiller, 15 placed), Fridge_01 (428), FridgeSmall_01 and Freezer_01; all are static visuals with no open action, so any take-from-fridge behaviour is LF script.
4. Bar: BarTable_01 top 1.04 m carries BarTap_01 (its ActionsManagerComponent has a context but no action entries), GlassBeer_01/02, BeerBottle_01 variants; Stool_01/02/StoolPiano_01 (0.50 m seat, no sit action) and BeerKegMetal_01 behind the bar.
5. Open-air trade: MarketStand_01 / _Umbrella (destruction disabled), Crate_01 colour variants, BasketWicker_01 and NewspaperStand_01 as produce and press props; ShoppingBasket_01 and ShoppingCart_01 at the entrance.

## Gaps and caveats

- No vending machine, ATM, gondola or glass display counter prefab exists under Props/Commercial in the installed game (asset_search "vending" found nothing); Bookshelf_02 (glass) and CoolingBoxShop_01 are the nearest display pieces.
- No prop here has a working user action: the only ActionsManagerComponent is on BarTap_01 and lists a context only. No storage components were found on any of these prefabs.
- "Destr. yes" means SCR_DestructionMultiPhaseComponent is present and not set Enabled 0 in the prefab chain; the parent .ct default is binary and could not be read.
- Per-colour/size variants (CoatRack_02-05, CabinetMetal_01 variants, CrateWooden_02 other sizes, Notice/Poster series) are not listed individually.
- Prices and place names must not be baked into props: core text is neutral (AGENTS.md rule 7).
