## What a furnished vanilla Everon house contains (from world.txt counts)

Whole-map totals of non-structure entities (all variants of the family, not only the rows above):

- Sleeping: 1351 beds (Bed, BedDouble, BunkBed) with 584 nightstands, 993 wardrobes and 934 dressers.
- Seating: 3830 chairs (Chair_01/02, Old, Office, Recreation, School), 801 armchairs, 446 sofas, plus kitchen, wooden and street benches.
- Tables: about 2460 (Table_01/02, Dining, Kitchen, Small, Old, Coffee), 502 carpets, 501 TVs, 302 radios, 421 phone stands.
- Kitchen: 369 stoves, 472 fridges, 405 Kitchen_01/02 counter blocks, 871 sinks with 1313 faucets, 311 washing machines.
- Bathroom: 569 toilets, 419 bathtubs, 469 showers, 737 bathroom mirrors.
- Dressing: 8219 curtain pieces, 2750 paintings, 2934 room plants, 1933 ceiling lights plus 2064 wall lights.

Per-town samples (450 m radius) follow the same proportions: Saint Philippe is the biggest (for example 60 Bed_01 and 150 Wardrobe_01 against 40 and 79 in Saint Pierre). Morton has no Nightstand_01. Fireplace_01 is rare in towns (8 in all four samples).

Facts that matter for fit-out:

- Most props are plain meshes with no vanilla action. Verified by reading each `.et` and its base prefab: the only user actions found are `SCR_FlushToilet` on `Toilet_01`, `SCR_LightFireplaceUserAction` on `Civilian/Fireplace_01`, four `SCR_PlayInstrument` tunes on `Piano_01` (inherited from `Piano_01_Base`) and `SCR_TurnOnAction` (a radio broadcast) on `RadioSmall_01` (from `RadioSmall_01_Base`). `Faucet_01` carries an ActionsManagerComponent context but no action entry. No sit, sleep, open, cook or wash action exists on any of them.
- No prop in this list has an inventory storage component (BenchStreet_01_green and ShelfDecorative base were not individually checked). Wardrobes, fridges, dressers and cupboards are not openable.
- LF sit support (F key, `LF_SitAction`, Feature/Emote) exists only through wrapper prefabs in `addons/LifeFramework/Prefabs/Props/Seats/`: `LF_Seat_Chair_02_green`, `LF_Seat_BenchStreet_01_green`, `LF_Seat_BenchStreet_01_red`, `LF_Seat_BenchStreet_02`. The wrappers inherit the vanilla prefab and add an ActionsManagerComponent with one `LF_SitAction` per place; any other seat (Chair_01, sofas, armchairs, stools) needs a new wrapper with its own `m_vSeatOffset`.
- Beds can become respawn points with `LF_HospitalBedComponent` (Prefabs/Medic/LF_FirstSpawnBed.et is the first-night variant); a rest or sleep action does not exist.
- All entries inherit the destruction base (`SCR_DestructionMultiPhaseComponent`) except Stool_01 and Carpet_01 (Props_Base only, not destructible). Dressing a house where props must survive gunfire or vehicles needs a non-destructible variant.
- Placement of furniture inside vanilla buildings is not verified here (positions are world-space entities at floor height, for example Chair_01 at y 1.85 in Saint Philippe); not checked against building footprints.
