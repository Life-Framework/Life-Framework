# Atmosphere (music and background sound)

- ✅ **Ambient emitters (2026-11-13).** `LF_AmbientEmitterComponent` (`Feature/Atmosphere`) is a looped positional sound on a prop or an area entity: sound project, start radius, stop margin, priority, group, hours of the day (local solar time) and an optional `LF_AmbientCondition`. Every machine that renders runs `LF_Atmosphere`: a 500 ms tick reads each emitter's distance to the listener (`AudioSystem.GetDistance`) and `LF_AtmosphereMix.Select` picks which sound: in range, allowed, one per group, at most `s_iMaxVoices` (6), higher priority then nearer. Voices go through `SCR_SoundManagerModule`, so a source inside a building is muffled outside. A dedicated server runs none of it. Logs `[LFDebug:Atmosphere] start|voice up|stop|mix`. Tests: `atmosphere/mix` (LOGIC), `atmosphere/switch` (WORLD), `atmosphere-selftest` in `tools\cli validate`, `session.mjs atmosphere-proof` (needs `session up --profile full --sound`).
- ✅ **Radio switch.** `LF_AmbientSwitchComponent` is the only replicated state: one `RplProp` bool, written by the server in `SetOn` (the radio's `LF_AmbientSwitchAction`, "Switch off" / "Switch on", anyone may use it) and `Pulse(seconds)`. `LF_ProcessAction` calls `LF_AmbientSwitchComponent.PulseOn` after a successful process (`m_fRunSoundSeconds`, 12), so the sawmill is heard running after each log. ⚠️ Not persisted: a radio switched off is on again after a restart.

- ✅ **Radio playlists (#1969, 2026-11-16).** A radio rotates through a list instead of looping one track. `LF_RadioPlaylist` (`Feature/Atmosphere/LF_RadioPlaylist.c`) is a list of `LF_RadioTrack` (project, seconds) keyed by the emitter's sound slot, so no prefab changes: a modded `LF_AmbientEmitterComponent` plays the list's current track, moves on when the track's seconds are up (or the voice stops: a radio out of earshot carries on), wraps after the last, skips a track that does not load with one `LF_Debug.Error`, and is silent outside the list's hours (`m_fHourFrom`/`m_fHourTo`, may wrap midnight). - 🔧 **Placed bird flocks wake when a player walks up (2026-11-23, #4403; merged, in-dev).** The pigeons and gulls placed by the square and harbor slots never showed because a vanilla `SCR_Bird` flock sleeps with every bird hidden until something calls `AIAnimal.ManualActivation()`; the world's `AIBirdManagerComponent` does that for the flocks it spawns, nothing did it for a flock a layer places. `LF_FlockActivation.c` (`Feature/Atmosphere`): the server registers every named (layer-placed) flock, and when a player's character comes within 40 m wakes it once: the birds appear, burst up and fly one loop, land, and are hidden again (the engine has no perched-and-visible state, so a flock cannot "sit on the dock"; the earlier note that a flock lifts off by itself at 50 m was wrong). The flock re-arms once every player is farther than 80 m, at least 45 s after its last wake, so someone standing on the square is not showered every minute but one who leaves and comes back sees the birds again. Not at world start: the wake is one broadcast and a client still loading when it is sent never shows the birds. The manager's own (unnamed) flocks are left alone. Logs `[LFDebug:Birds] flock <name> woken at <pos>` (server). Harness verb `flocks [radius]` (client, `LF_FlockHarness.c`) replies what this client holds (`flocks=N birds=M birdsVisible=V`, each flock's distance and visible flag, each bird's height above ground and visible flag); `square-harbor-life-proof` uses it. Test `atmosphere/flock-activation` (LOGIC, the rules; the grid has no AIWorld, so a flock cannot wake in a test run). Proved on the island 2026-11-23: `birdsVisible` 0 before the walk-up, all of a flock's birds after, gulls in the picture above the apron.
