# Atmosphere (music, background sound and night light)

> **In short.** Places have their own sound: pub music, a furnace, a sawmill after a cut, hens on the farm, work-site whistles and bells, all heard only when you are near and quieter through walls. At night the town now has light: street lamps along the roads, lamps over shop and building fronts so their signs can be read, and warm glows in the windows, switched on by the world clock in the evening and off in the morning. The outpost camp has its own lanterns and fire. This is built and in the current build; it is still being playtested, and some dark corners remain. The rest of this page is the engineering record.

- ✅ **Ambient emitters (2026-11-13).** `LF_AmbientEmitterComponent` (`Feature/Atmosphere`) is a looped positional sound on a prop or an area entity: sound project, start radius, stop margin, priority, group, hours of the day (local solar time) and an optional `LF_AmbientCondition`. Every machine that renders runs `LF_Atmosphere`: a 500 ms tick reads each emitter's distance to the listener (`AudioSystem.GetDistance`) and `LF_AtmosphereMix.Select` picks which sound: in range, allowed, one per group, at most `s_iMaxVoices` (6), higher priority then nearer. Voices go through `SCR_SoundManagerModule`, so a source inside a building is muffled outside. A dedicated server runs none of it. Logs `[LFDebug:Atmosphere] start|voice up|stop|mix`. Tests: `atmosphere/mix` (LOGIC), `atmosphere/switch` (WORLD), `atmosphere-selftest` in `tools\cli validate`, `session.mjs atmosphere-proof` (needs `session up --profile full --sound`).
- ✅ **Radio switch.** `LF_AmbientSwitchComponent` is the only replicated state: one `RplProp` bool, written by the server in `SetOn` (the radio's `LF_AmbientSwitchAction`, "Switch off" / "Switch on", anyone may use it) and `Pulse(seconds)`. `LF_ProcessAction` calls `LF_AmbientSwitchComponent.PulseOn` after a successful process (`m_fRunSoundSeconds`, 12), so the sawmill is heard running after each log. ⚠️ Not persisted: a radio switched off is on again after a restart.

- 🔧 **Placed bird flocks wake when a player walks up (2026-11-23, #4403; merged, in-dev).** The pigeons and gulls placed by the square and harbor slots never showed because a vanilla `SCR_Bird` flock sleeps with every bird hidden until something calls `AIAnimal.ManualActivation()`; the world's `AIBirdManagerComponent` does that for the flocks it spawns, nothing did it for a flock a layer places. `LF_FlockActivation.c` (`Feature/Atmosphere`): the server registers every named (layer-placed) flock, and when a player's character comes within 40 m wakes it once: the birds appear, burst up and fly one loop, land, and are hidden again (the engine has no perched-and-visible state, so a flock cannot "sit on the dock"; the earlier note that a flock lifts off by itself at 50 m was wrong). The flock re-arms once every player is farther than 80 m, at least 45 s after its last wake, so someone standing on the square is not showered every minute but one who leaves and comes back sees the birds again. Not at world start: the wake is one broadcast and a client still loading when it is sent never shows the birds. The manager's own (unnamed) flocks are left alone. Logs `[LFDebug:Birds] flock <name> woken at <pos>` (server). Harness verb `flocks [radius]` (client, `LF_FlockHarness.c`) replies what this client holds (`flocks=N birds=M birdsVisible=V`, each flock's distance and visible flag, each bird's height above ground and visible flag); `square-harbor-life-proof` uses it. Test `atmosphere/flock-activation` (LOGIC, the rules; the grid has no AIWorld, so a flock cannot wake in a test run). Proved on the island 2026-11-23: `birdsVisible` 0 before the walk-up, all of a flock's birds after, gulls in the picture above the apron.
