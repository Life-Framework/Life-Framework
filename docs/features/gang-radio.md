# Gang radio (#4215)

- ✅ Gang bands (GA-C7, #4215, LOGIC `comms/gang-radio-band`): `LF_GangRadio.BandFor(orgId)` gives an organization its own hidden band on the native R148 radio, a random free channel from `Configs/Comms/LF_GangRadio.conf` (44000 to 54000 kHz in 200 kHz steps, 51 channels; the R148 factory channel 38000 and the police and medic service frequencies are never given) plus a fresh encryption key, so a rival who dials the frequency by hand hears nothing. Two gangs never hold one band. The band goes back to the free set when the organization loses its last member (`LF_OnOrganizationEmptied`, the disband) and only then can another gang get it, with a new key. Server only, in memory: after a restart a gang is assigned again on first use and the radio item retunes. With every channel held a new gang is refused with an `[LFDebug:GangRadio]` error. Not done: dropping the channel on leave (GA-C9).
- ✅ Gang radio item and "Tune to gang channel" (GA-C8, #4221, WORLD `comms/gang-radio-tune`): `LF_GangRadio` (pack, `Prefabs/Items/Comms/LF_GangRadio.et`) is a copy of the native R148. A gang member carrying it picks "Tune to gang channel" from its action list; the server checks the carrier and the membership, then sets the band's frequency and encryption key and switches the set on (`LF_GangRadioTune.ServerTune`). Members on the band hear each other through vanilla radio voice; an outsider who dials the frequency has no key and hears nothing. Someone outside a gang sees the action greyed out with "Gang members only". When a member leaves or is kicked (`LF_OnOrganizationMemberLeft`), the band is drawn again with a new frequency and key: the radios of online members move over, the leaver's radio comes off the band, and a member who was offline retunes from the action list. The band lives in memory, so after a restart every radio retunes the same way (a stale frequency or key is overwritten). The $60 sale row also sits at the Saint-Philippe gun counter (#4703, `gang_radio` in `LF_Catalogue_Clothing_Everon1989.conf`, open to anyone, no licence), so a crew has a radio before it finds an outpost. Not done: the three-client session proof.
