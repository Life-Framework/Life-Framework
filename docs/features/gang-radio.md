# Gang radio (#4215)

- ✅ Gang bands (GA-C7, #4215, LOGIC `comms/gang-radio-band`): `LF_GangRadio.BandFor(orgId)` gives an organization its own hidden band on the native R148 radio, a random free channel from `Configs/Comms/LF_GangRadio.conf` (44000 to 54000 kHz in 200 kHz steps, 51 channels; the R148 factory channel 38000 and the police and medic service frequencies are never given) plus a fresh encryption key, so a rival who dials the frequency by hand hears nothing. Two gangs never hold one band. The band goes back to the free set when the organization loses its last member (`LF_OnOrganizationEmptied`, the disband) and only then can another gang get it, with a new key. Server only, in memory: after a restart a gang is assigned again on first use and the radio item retunes. With every channel held a new gang is refused with an `[LFDebug:GangRadio]` error. Not done: the radio item and "Tune to gang channel" (GA-C8), dropping the channel on leave (GA-C9).
