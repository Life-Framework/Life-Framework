### `LF_ImplementHitch` and `LF_ImplementComponent` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_ImplementHitch.c`, `LF_ImplementComponent.c` (#3284, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A reaper hitches behind a UAZ469 or a Z7211. Server `LF_ImplementHitch.Hitch(vehicle, out reason)` spawns `Prefabs/Harvest/LF_Reaper_01.et` with the vehicle as its parent (`EntitySpawnParams.Parent`, the vanilla weapon-rack way) at the local offset of the vehicle's row in `Configs/Harvest/LF_Hitches.conf`; a row matches the vehicle's prefab or any ancestor, so every UAZ469 variant uses the `UAZ469_base` row. Neither mesh has a hitch pivot: the UAZ469 offset (0, 0, -3.4) sits behind its measured rear bound (-1.87 m); the Z7211 row (0, 0, -3) is an estimate, since the Rally DLC tractor does not load on the test server. The reaper has a mesh (vanilla `CultivatorWreck_01` until H5b), a `Hierarchy` and an `RplComponent`, and no physics body, so it never drags or flips the vehicle; the hierarchy replicates, so other players and late joiners get it under the same vehicle. Refusals (not the server, not on the hitch list, already towing, a bad prefab) set the reason and log on `[LFDebug:Harvest]`. `Unhitch` deletes the reaper; `FindHitched` finds it on any machine. `LF_ImplementComponent` holds the bar width and the replicated hopper kg (`SetHopperKg`, for the cut tick #3289) and logs `implement present` / `implement follows` (every 10 m the vehicle moves) on every machine, with `behind true|false`. No player action hitches yet (the implement rack is H8, #3295); nothing is persisted. Test: `harvest/implement-hitch` (WORLD). Session proof: `session hitch-sync` (harness verb `hitch`, `LF_ImplementHitchHarness.c`): a late joiner sees the reaper behind the UAZ after it moves 50 m, and it goes when the vehicle is deleted the garage way.

### `LF_WheatFieldReplication` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_WheatFieldReplication.c` (#3288, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A field's cut cells reach every client and a late joiner. The server sends cuts in batches: at most one reliable `RpcDo_LF_WheatCut` per field per 250 ms (leading edge sends at once, the rest wait for the next allowed frame), carrying packed cell words as one string (12 hex characters per bitset word: 4 of word index, 8 of mask), plus one `RpcDo_LF_WheatRegrow`; a cell already cut is never queued, so a repeat cut sends nothing. `RplSave/RplLoad` on the field carries the bitset (260 cells are 9 ints) and the client redraws from it. A dedicated server draws no patch. The replicated side is a `modded class LF_WheatField` (pure parts: `LF_WheatFieldWire`, `LF_WheatCutBatch`). Harness verb `wheatsync state|cut|regrow <fieldIndex>` (dev gate). Tests: `harvest/wheat-field-snapshot` (LOGIC); proof: dedicated server, two clients and a late joiner each report 21 stubbles after a 21-cell cut.

### Wheat and stubble patch meshes — `Prefabs/Harvest/LF_WheatPatch_01.et`, `LF_WheatStubble_01.et`, `Assets/Harvest/WheatPatch/` (#3290, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 The two 3 x 3 m meshes a wheat field draws per cell. `LF_WheatPatch_01` is 75 alpha-cut planes (25 positions, a star of three crossed planes at each, bent at half height) about 1.1 m tall: 300 triangles at LOD0, 72 at LOD1. `LF_WheatStubble_01` is the foot of the same clusters cut level at 30 cm: 216 and 72 triangles. Both use the base game's own wheat atlas, `Assets/Vegetation/_Polyplanes/polyplane_wheat_01_{A,BCR,NTC}.edds` (no texture of ours), through two `MatPBRTreeCrown` materials modelled on the vanilla `PotatoCrop_01` (`LF_WheatPatch.emat` sways in the wind, `LF_WheatStubble.emat` does not). No collider, no shadow. The cards overhang the 3 m square by about 10 cm, so patches overlap a little and tile with no seam at any yaw (the field view turns each cell by a pseudo-random angle). Built by `node tools/blender/harvest/build.mjs` (Blender script `wheat_patch.py`, then `tools\cli rebuild-fbx` for the `.xob`); `Configs/Harvest/LF_WheatFields.conf` points at both prefabs. DebugWorld grid cell `slice-3290` shows a 4 x 4 block (12 wheat, 4 stubble). Test: `harvest/wheat-patch-meshes` (WORLD). Credit: the textures are Bohemia Interactive's, shipped with the game.

### `LF_OwnedImplements`, `LF_HarvestSavePayload` and the implement rack — `Feature/Harvest/LF_OwnedImplements.c`, `LF_OwnedImplementsSavePayload.c`, `LF_ImplementRackAction.c`, `Prefabs/Harvest/LF_ImplementRack.et` (#3295, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A player buys a reaper once and keeps it on a list. The farm supply counter (catalogue outlet `farm_supply`, offer `farm_reaper`, $1,200, `LF_Everon1989/Configs/Catalogue/LF_Catalogue_Kit_Everon1989/LF_Catalogue_FarmSupply.conf`) hands the sale to `LF_OwnedImplements.ShopBuy` (a three-line hook in `LF_ShopComponent.Buy`), which charges cash and puts `reaper` on the buyer's owned list instead of the inventory; a second reaper is refused (`LF_EShopRefusal.ALREADY_OWNED`, "You already own one of these"). The list is kept per character record key and saved in the `harvest` section (`LF_HarvestSavePayload`, version 1, one `LF_OwnedImplementRow` per implement). The rack prefab has two prompts, *Take out reaper* and *Put back reaper* (`LF_ImplementRackAction`). Take-out hitches the player's own reaper (`LF_ImplementHitch.Hitch`) to the nearest hitch-list vehicle within 8 m of the rack that tows nothing and is not saved as another character's; a player who owns none, or whose reaper is already out, is refused with a toast. Put-back needs the towing vehicle within 8 m of the rack, dumps the hopper (a warning toast says how many kg of grain were lost; the hopper is never saved or paid out) and deletes the reaper. What is out is session state only, so a reaper that is out at a restart is back on the list and not in the world; a reaper whose vehicle is destroyed is back on the list too. The farm supply counter itself is not placed yet (the rack and counter go in the world with the Saint-Philippe farm pass). Tests: `harvest/implement-rack` (WORLD), `harvest/persist-owned-implements` (PERSISTENCE), `everon/farm-supply` (pack leg).

### `LF_ReaperCutTick` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_ReaperCutTick.c` (#3289, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A hitched reaper cuts the field under it and fills its hopper. `LF_ImplementComponent` registers each reaper that spawns on the server; a 200 ms server loop reads the towing vehicle's physics speed and heading, finds the `LF_WheatField` holding the bar (cached until the bar leaves it), and applies the H2 rules: 0.5 to 6 m/s cuts the cells under the bar (`LF_WheatGrid.Footprint`, width from the implement, 1 m long) through `LF_WheatField.CutCells` and adds cells times 10 kg to the replicated hopper (600 kg cap; a cell is cut only if its yield fits). Too fast, stopped and hopper full are each logged (`reaper N refused: TOO_FAST ...`) and shown to the driver once per change (`#LF-Harvest_TooFast`, `#LF-Harvest_HopperFull`; stopped has no hint). The hopper is session-only like the implement: nothing is saved, and no player can hitch a reaper until the rack (H8, #3295); the unload (H7, #3294) and the rack must persist or pay out the grain. The 10 m "implement follows" lines are debug level now. Test: `harvest/reaper-cut` (WORLD). Session proof: `hitch fieldspawn`, `hitch drive`, `hitch state` (grid cell slice-3287; `state` prints `hopper=` and `cut=`).

### `LF_GranaryUnload` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_GranaryUnload.c` (#3294, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 Unload a reaper's hopper at the weighbridge. `LF_GranaryUnloadAction` is a prompt for the weighbridge entity (mode *Sell grain* or *Bag grain*, unload box = half extents in the entity's local frame, default 6 x 4 x 10 m). It shows to the driver of a vehicle that tows a reaper with grain in the hopper and stands in the box. The server decides everything from the user's own seat (`LF_GranaryUnload.Sell` / `Bag`); the client sends only the prompt. *Sell grain* pays the market's wholesale curve for the hopper's whole 50 kg lots of `LF_GrainSack` (`LF_MarketBook.QuoteAt`, price moves down per lot, `LF_OnMarketPriceMoved`) through `LF_MoneyUtils.AddCash` with reason `grain-sale` (so `LF_OnMoneyMoved` and the ledger record it, cash drops at the feet if the pockets are full); the hopper is emptied first and put back if the payout fails; a part lot (under 50 kg) stays in the hopper. Grain the market does not price (market off, no row; the granary row is the pack's `LF_Market_Granary_Everon1989.conf`) is refused with a toast. *Bag grain* fills grain sacks into the vehicle storage (`LF_InventoryUtils.AddAmount`) and takes out of the hopper exactly what went in; what does not fit stays. Refusals (outside the box, no reaper, empty, passenger, on foot, no room, no price) log `grain unload refused (<why>)` and toast the player; rate limited like a purchase. **Not in this card:** the prompt is not on any entity yet; the weighbridge `WeightStation_01` gets it with the placement card (H12). Test: `harvest/granary-unload` (WORLD).

### `LF_HarvestContract` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_HarvestContract.c` (#3297, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 The farm board's harvest contract, server API only. `Post(field)` posts one contract per ripe field (a field already cut to 95 % is refused). `Take(field, user, rental, dlcOwned, kitAt, kitAngles, out kit)` takes it: with the player's own reaper (they must own one, hitch it at the rack) or with a rental kit, which charges a $300 deposit (`harvest-deposit`) and spawns a Z7211 when the taker's client says `DlcManager.CanUseResource` passes (`LF_VehicleDlcGate.LocalOwns`, the server cannot ask), otherwise a UAZ469, with a reaper hitched; the kit is a job vehicle, kept out of the world save and never an owned record. One taker per field. `Settle(field, now)` (a 5 s server sweep while a contract is taken) pays at 95 % cut: a $40 bonus (`harvest-bonus`) and FARMER XP, 5 per whole 100 kg cut since the contract was taken (`LF_JobLevels.AddXp`, the daily FARMER cap applies), once. `ReturnKit(field, user, at, out refunded)` takes the kit back within 8 m of the board, refunds the deposit once and deletes the kit (grain left in the hopper is lost); returned before the line, the contract opens again. A kit kept past 30 minutes lapses: it is deleted, the deposit stays with the farm and the field is free again. Contracts are session state; anyone seated in a kit is put out before it is deleted (a seat that stays taken retries every 5 s). Open deposits are the save section `harvest_deposits` (`LF_HarvestContractSavePayload`): a restart with a kit out pays the deposit into the taker's bank on the next boot, once (`harvest-deposit-restart-refund`). The board prop and its prompts are `LF_HarvestBoard` (#3656, below). Test: `harvest/harvest-contract` (WORLD).
