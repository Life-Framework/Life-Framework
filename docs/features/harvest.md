### `LF_WheatField` and `LF_WheatFieldView` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_WheatField.c`, `LF_WheatFieldView.c` (#3287, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A placed wheat field draws itself: `Prefabs/Harvest/LF_WheatField.et` (`LF_WheatField`, box `m_fSizeX` x `m_fSizeZ`, default 60 x 40 m = 260 cells, centred on the entity, yaw ignored) keeps the `LF_WheatGrid` cut bitset and, on every machine except a dedicated server, one local `StaticModelEntity` patch per standing cell (`SpawnEntityPrefabLocal`, never replicated). `CutCells(cells)` marks the bitset and swaps each cut patch for stubble (a repeat or out-of-range cell is skipped, the return is the newly cut count); `Regrow()` clears the bitset and stands every patch again. The patch, stubble and cell size come from `Configs/Harvest/LF_WheatFields.conf` (the wheat and stubble patch meshes of #3290; the vanilla stand-ins `c_arrhenatherum_elatius_01` and `c_fallen_leaves_01` are gone). Every change logs on `[LFDebug:Harvest]`. Nothing replicates yet (#3288) and no reaper calls it (#3289). DebugWorld grid cell `slice-3287` holds a 45 x 21 m field. Test: `harvest/wheat-field-cut` (WORLD).

### `LF_WheatFieldReplication` — `Feature/Harvest/LF_WheatFieldReplication.c` (#3288, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 A field's cut cells reach every client and a late joiner. The server sends cuts in batches: at most one reliable `RpcDo_LF_WheatCut` per field per 250 ms (leading edge sends at once, the rest wait for the next allowed frame), carrying packed cell words as one string (12 hex characters per bitset word: 4 of word index, 8 of mask), plus one `RpcDo_LF_WheatRegrow`; a cell already cut is never queued, so a repeat cut sends nothing. `RplSave/RplLoad` on the field carries the bitset (260 cells are 9 ints) and the client redraws from it. A dedicated server draws no patch. The replicated side is a `modded class LF_WheatField` (pure parts: `LF_WheatFieldWire`, `LF_WheatCutBatch`). Harness verb `wheatsync state|cut|regrow <fieldIndex>` (dev gate). Tests: `harvest/wheat-field-snapshot` (LOGIC); proof: dedicated server, two clients and a late joiner each report 21 stubbles after a 21-cell cut.

### Wheat and stubble patch meshes — `Prefabs/Harvest/LF_WheatPatch_01.et`, `LF_WheatStubble_01.et`, `Assets/Harvest/WheatPatch/` (#3290, 2026-11-19)
- 🧪 The two 3 x 3 m meshes a wheat field draws per cell. `LF_WheatPatch_01` is 75 alpha-cut planes (25 positions, a star of three crossed planes at each, bent at half height) about 1.1 m tall: 300 triangles at LOD0, 72 at LOD1. `LF_WheatStubble_01` is the foot of the same clusters cut level at 30 cm: 216 and 72 triangles. Both use the base game's own wheat atlas, `Assets/Vegetation/_Polyplanes/polyplane_wheat_01_{A,BCR,NTC}.edds` (no texture of ours), through two `MatPBRTreeCrown` materials modelled on the vanilla `PotatoCrop_01` (`LF_WheatPatch.emat` sways in the wind, `LF_WheatStubble.emat` does not). No collider, no shadow. The cards overhang the 3 m square by about 10 cm, so patches overlap a little and tile with no seam at any yaw (the field view turns each cell by a pseudo-random angle). Built by `node tools/blender/harvest/build.mjs` (Blender script `wheat_patch.py`, then `tools\cli rebuild-fbx` for the `.xob`); `Configs/Harvest/LF_WheatFields.conf` points at both prefabs. DebugWorld grid cell `slice-3290` shows a 4 x 4 block (12 wheat, 4 stubble). Test: `harvest/wheat-patch-meshes` (WORLD). Credit: the textures are Bohemia Interactive's, shipped with the game.
