# Qualifications

### `LF_QualificationRules`, `LF_QualificationConfig` — `Feature/Qualifications/`, `Configs/Qualifications/LF_Qualifications.conf` (#3424, 2026-11-19)
- A course (id, faction, minimum rank, fee, study time, unlock ids) is config, and a pure rule says whether a character may take it: the wrong faction, too low a rank, a course already held and a short purse are each refused with their own localization key (`LF-Qual_*`), and a pass returns the unlock ids (strings such as `detective`, `traffic`), the fee and the study time. The core ships four neutral courses; a pack adds, replaces, patches or removes courses by id through an overlay. Nothing takes money, runs the study or saves a held course yet. Tests: `qualifications/rules` (LOGIC).

### `LF_Qualifications`, `LF_QualificationsSavePayload` — `Feature/Qualifications/` (#3425, 2026-11-19)
- The courses each character holds, keyed by the character's record key: `Grant(character, courseId)` adds a course the table lists (an unknown or already-held course is refused and logged), `Has(character, unlock)` answers by unlock id through the course table, and `GetHeld` gives the held ids for `LF_QualificationRules.Check`. Held courses survive a restart in the `qualifications` save section; a saved course the table no longer lists is dropped on load and logged, and a boot whose save predates the section starts with nobody holding a course. Nothing calls `Grant` yet: the course desk does. Tests: `qualifications/restart` (PERSISTENCE, with a `wt e2e` restart check).

### `LF_TrainingDesk`, `LF_TrainingDeskAction` — `Feature/Qualifications/`, `Prefabs/Props/Police/LF_TrainingDesk.et` (#3426, 2026-11-19)
- At the training desk an officer on duty picks a course (one held action per shipped course; the hold is the study, a quarter of a second per in-game minute, so the detective course is a 60 s hold). When the hold completes the server checks the course rules, takes the fee from carried cash (a cost to the officer, never a raise), grants the course and toasts "You've qualified: Detective". Off duty, the wrong faction or rank, a held course and a short purse are each refused with a toast and no charge. The fee is taken at completion, not at the start of the hold, because the start of a hold only runs on the acting client: leaving mid-study charges and grants nothing. The desk lists the four shipped courses; a pack course added by overlay has no action until the pack adds one. Tests: `qualifications/training-desk` (WORLD). Look: `grid:training-desk`.

### `LF_QualificationGate` — `Feature/Qualifications/` (#3427, 2026-11-19)
- A qualification now means something. The quick vehicle search stays open to every on-duty officer; the new "Full search" row (`LF_VehicleSearchFullAction`, the quick search and the car's hidden compartment too) and "Pin evidence" on the case board (opening a case) need the `detective` unlock. On the server the action asks `LF_QualificationGate.Allows(character, action)` and an officer without the course gets "That's detective work. The training desk runs the course." and nothing happens. Unpinning stays open to every officer. The two searches keep separate repeat cooldowns. The "Full search" row sits next to "Search vehicle" on the ten vehicle prefabs that carry it. Tests: `qualifications/gate` (LOGIC).
