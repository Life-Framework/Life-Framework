### `LF_PriceCurve` — `Feature/Market/LF_PriceCurve.c` (#2844, framework #762)
- ✅ Pure price-curve rules in whole cash units (merged, in-dev). A good has a base, floor and ceiling, a drop per unit sold (`dropPerUnitPct` of the base, at least 1 unit when above 0) and a recovery per real hour (`recoverPctPerHour` of the gap to the base, at least 1 unit, never past the base). `PriceAfterSale` never goes below the floor; `BulkTotal` prices each unit marginally, so a bulk sale of N pays the sum of N single sales; a drop of 0 keeps today's fixed price. `Recover` pays whole real hours only and counts a gap past `maxRecoveryHours` as the cap (`IsClamped` tells the caller to move its stamp to now). All int maths through `LF_Utils.MaxInt/MinInt/ClampInt` (#2787). Static, no world, no state. Test `market/price-curve` (LOGIC).
- ✅ (2026-11-18, #3028) The price is kept in hundredths of a cash unit, so a cheap good no longer drops a whole 1 per unit: a 2% drop on a 5-cash apple is 0.10 a unit (ten apples leave it at 4, about 25 take it to half; before, it hit its floor of 3 after two). `LF_MarketBook` stores `m_iFine` and quotes whole cash rounded half up (the board shows what a single unit pays); a bulk sale pays the exact marginal sum in hundredths rounded once (ten 5-cash apples at 2%: 5.00 + 4.90 + ... + 4.10 = 45.50, paid 46). Recovery closes its percent of the gap in hundredths and a gap of one hundredth still closes, so a price reaches the base to the cent a few hours later than before. Config is unchanged (whole cash); a ceiling above 10,000,000 is refused at load. Sums stay exact above 2^24 and stop at `INT_MAX` (`MAX_BULK_UNITS` is now 60000). The sale notice reads bands from the rounded cash price, the same number the board shows. Test `market/price-curve` (LOGIC), `market/trader-sale` (WORLD).

### `LF_MarketBook` — `Feature/Market/LF_MarketBook.c`, `LF_MarketEvents.c`, `Feature/Trader/LF_TraderSale.c` (#2845)
- ✅ A trader sale rides the curve (merged, in-dev). Server-only, in-memory state per good: the current price and `lastSaleUnix`; a good never sold stands at its base. Recovery is applied lazily on each quote from the stamp (no tick), so a quote never moves the price; a sale moves the stamp by the whole hours recovery counted (to now on a first sale or past the cap), so steady trade still recovers. `LF_TraderSale.Sell` asks `LF_MarketBook.QuoteAt` for a good the market lists while `m_bEnabled` is on: the stack pays the curve's marginal bulk total (ledger `trader-sell` as before) and the price drops for the next seller; any other good, or the market off, pays the trader's fixed value exactly as before. Order: pay, move the price, take the item; a failed removal takes the cash back and restores the price. A completed curved sale publishes `LF_OnMarketPriceMoved(good, old, new)`. A client sale request now also counts against the `PURCHASE` rate limit and refuses a dead or downed seller (`SELLER_DOWN`). Saved since #2846 (below). Test `market/trader-sale` (WORLD).

### `LF_StallRules` — `Feature/Market/LF_StallRules.c`, `LF_StallConfig.c`, `Configs/Market/LF_Stalls.conf` (#2924, framework #786, #759)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) Stall market rules as pure server logic: no world, no RPC, no save, no statics; the core ships them **off** (`m_bEnabled` 0). The caller owns the cash and moves it only when a rule answers `LF_EStallResult.OK`. Market day is a config window of the in-game week (`m_iMarketDay` 0..6, `m_iOpenHour`..`m_iCloseHour`; the week is `dayNumber % 7`). `Rent` takes the pitch fee (`m_iPitchFee`) for that one market day and refuses outside the window, with a short purse, or twice; a pitch is for one market day and a new one clears the unreserved old listings. `List` puts one item at the holder's price on a rented stall: a price outside `m_iMinPrice..m_iMaxPrice` or past `m_iMaxListings` refuses. `Reserve` holds a buyer's cash in an `LF_StallEscrow` for the listing (no double reserve, no self-buy, market open); `Settle` pays the seller the price minus `m_iCutPct` (cut rounded down, the market keeps it) and removes the listing, `Refund` returns the whole amount and re-opens the listing; an escrow ends once, so a second settle, a refund after a settle (or the reverse) and another stall's attempt refuse. Money maths is int only through `LF_Utils.MaxInt/MinInt/ClampInt` (#2787); the cut is split so a price at `MAX_CASH` cannot overflow and running totals stop at `MAX_TOTAL`. `LF_StallConfig.Sanitize` switches a bad table off with `LF_Debug.Error("Market", ...)`. Test `market/stall-rules` (LOGIC). Not built yet: the stall prop and screens, taking the cash, the save, and the market-day clock that supplies `dayNumber` and `hour`.

### `LF_StallTrade` / `LF_StallBuyAction` — `Feature/Market/LF_StallListing.c`, `LF_StallBuyAction.c` (#2926, framework #786, #759)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, draft PR, REVIEW-NEEDED: money and items) List goods on a rented stall and buy them. **A listing is a claim, not custody:** the item stays in the holder's inventory and the listing is its price tag, so a restart, a crash or a holder logging out cannot lose an item, and at closing time unsold goods are simply still the holder's (no lost-and-found is needed). `LF_StallTrade.List` (server; holder only, within 5 m) refuses a price outside the config range, a full stall, and an item the holder's stock does not back (the stock must cover every listing of that prefab). `Buy` (server; buyer within 5 m) reserves the listing in `LF_StallRules` (no cash moved), checks the holder's character is in the world and still carries the item, takes the price from the buyer (ledger `stall_buy`), moves the holder's own item entity into the buyer's inventory (its rounds, damage, attachments and contents go with it; a counted stack moves one unit), then settles: the holder is paid the price minus the cut (`stall_sale`), the market keeps the cut. Each failure undoes the steps before it: no cash and the buyer is refunded and the listing re-opens; the holder took the item away, so the buyer is refunded and the stale listing drops (no dupe); the buyer has no room, so nothing moves and the cash goes back to the buyer. A listing with cash in escrow cannot be taken down. `LF_StallBuyAction` ("Buy from the stall") buys the cheapest open listing; the server toasts the buyer and the holder. Test `market/stall-trade` (WORLD). **Not built yet:** a listing screen (the holder lists and the buyer picks through the server API only), the list-an-item action with a price prompt, and saving the stall and its rent (a restart before closing frees the stall with no rent refund; there is no escrow to save, since a sale completes inside one server call).
