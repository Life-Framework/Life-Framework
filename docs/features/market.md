# Market (price curve)

Design context: `addons/LifeFramework/Scripts/Game/Feature/Market/Feature.md`.

### `LF_PriceCurve` — `Feature/Market/LF_PriceCurve.c` (#2844, framework #762)
- ✅ Pure price-curve rules in whole cash units (merged, in-dev). A good has a base, floor and ceiling, a drop per unit sold (`dropPerUnitPct` of the base, at least 1 unit when above 0) and a recovery per real hour (`recoverPctPerHour` of the gap to the base, at least 1 unit, never past the base). `PriceAfterSale` never goes below the floor; `BulkTotal` prices each unit marginally, so a bulk sale of N pays the sum of N single sales; a drop of 0 keeps today's fixed price. `Recover` pays whole real hours only and counts a gap past `maxRecoveryHours` as the cap (`IsClamped` tells the caller to move its stamp to now). All int maths through `LF_Utils.MaxInt/MinInt/ClampInt` (#2787). Static, no world, no state. Test `market/price-curve` (LOGIC).

### `LF_MarketConfig` — `Feature/Market/LF_MarketConfig.c`, `Configs/Market/LF_Market.conf` (#2844)
- ✅ Per good `m_sGood` (item prefab, the trader list's key), `m_iBase`, `m_iFloor`, `m_iCeiling`, `m_iDropPerUnitPct`, `m_iRecoverPctPerHour`; global `m_bEnabled` and `m_iMaxRecoveryHours` (168). The core ships the market **off** (`m_bEnabled` 0) with three neutral goods (copper bar, iron bar, gold bar), so no server changes behaviour until it opts in. `Sanitize` at load removes a good with a floor above the ceiling, a base outside them, a percent outside 0..100, a repeat or no prefab, each with `LF_Debug.Error("Market", ...)` naming the good. Nothing reads the curve yet: the per-good state, the save and the trader sale are later cards of the chapter.

### `LF_MarketBook` — `Feature/Market/LF_MarketBook.c`, `LF_MarketEvents.c`, `Feature/Trader/LF_TraderSale.c` (#2845)
- ✅ A trader sale rides the curve (merged, in-dev). Server-only, in-memory state per good: the current price and `lastSaleUnix`; a good never sold stands at its base. Recovery is applied lazily on each quote from the stamp (no tick), so a quote never moves the price; a sale moves the stamp by the whole hours recovery counted (to now on a first sale or past the cap), so steady trade still recovers. `LF_TraderSale.Sell` asks `LF_MarketBook.QuoteAt` for a good the market lists while `m_bEnabled` is on: the stack pays the curve's marginal bulk total (ledger `trader-sell` as before) and the price drops for the next seller; any other good, or the market off, pays the trader's fixed value exactly as before. Order: pay, move the price, take the item; a failed removal takes the cash back and restores the price. A completed curved sale publishes `LF_OnMarketPriceMoved(good, old, new)`. A client sale request now also counts against the `PURCHASE` rate limit and refuses a dead or downed seller (`SELLER_DOWN`). Not saved yet (#2846): a restart puts every price back at its base. Test `market/trader-sale` (WORLD).

### Profit-loop check reads the market curve — `tools/economy/profit-market.mjs` (#2848)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) `validate-profit-loops` fails a curved good whose cheapest shop buy price (shop price rules after the owner `shops` section, or a catalogue outlet) is below the highest `m_iCeiling` any market file gives it, the most a seller is ever paid. It reads the core `Configs/Market/LF_Market.conf` and every pack `addons/<pack>/Configs/Market/*.conf`, and the line names the shop file, the market file and the good. Fixture `tools/validation/fixtures/profit-loop/market-floor-loop` (buy 8 against a ceiling of 10 fails, a ceiling of 7 passes, a pack overlay ceiling counts), proof `profit-loop-selftest`. The shipped loop it found, GoldBar (shop 250, ceiling 280), was retuned: the core ceiling is now 240 (floor stays 120, half the ceiling), so nothing is baselined. A market ceiling must never exceed what any shop sells the good for. Not read: pack shop price overlays (`LF_ShopPrices_*.conf`), which price their own shop only.

### `LF_PriceBoard` - `Feature/Market/LF_PriceBoard.c`, `Prefabs/Props/Market/LF_PriceBoard.et` (#2847)
- Chalk price board at the trader (merged, in-dev). A board kind (`LF_BoardProviders`, kind `prices`): reading it asks the server, which builds the answer fresh from `LF_MarketBook` and replies once to that reader (the shared `RpcAsk_LF_Board` / `RpcDo_LF_ShowBoard` pair, so no new RPC, no `RplProp`, no JIP state). One line per curved good in config order with today's price and a `v` when below base (only the price and a below-base flag cross, never the base or curve; reads ride the shared per-player board read limit); a disabled market answers an empty board painted as *No prices today.* The prefab inherits `LF_Sign_Chalkboard.et` and adds `LF_PriceBoardReadAction`. The printed chalk face (a title and price lines on the board mesh) is not built yet. Test `market/price-board` (WORLD).
