# NPC dialogue scenes (spike, 2026-11-13)

### `LF_Dialogue` / `LF_DialogueScene` — `Scripts/Game/Feature/Dialogue/`

- 🟡 **Talking to an NPC is a scene.** The camera eases to the NPC and back, and keeps ONE
  framing for the whole talk (over the player's shoulder, the NPC from about the chest up: no zoom on a
  line, no cut); the line is a caption at the bottom with
  a recorded voice; replies are a numbered list (1 to 4, or up, down and confirm; Back leaves;
  confirm skips a line). Movement, view and weapon controls are held and the HUD is hidden
  for the length of it. Only the talker's client changes.
- The server owns the conversation (`LF_Dialogue`): the client sends an NPC, then a reply
  index; the server checks reach (4 m), that it offered that reply, and its condition again, runs
  the reply's action, and answers with node and line ids. Sessions are not saved.
- Data: `Configs/Dialogue/LF_Dialogue_<Name>.conf` (`LF_DialogueConfig`) on the NPC's
  `LF_DialogueComponent`, generated by `tools\cli asset dialogue <npc>` from
  `tools/assets/dialogue/<npc>/dialogue.json` (`docs/asset-pipeline/dialogue.md`).
- A reply's condition or action is a hook class `LF_DialogueHook_<Name>` in the owning feature;
  the quests have `QuestConverse`, `QuestTurnedIn`, `QuestNotTurnedIn`.
- Voice and lips (`LF_DialogueVoice`): `AudioSystem.PlayEvent` from the NPC's head; the lip
  lips and jaw follow the line's loudness envelope, smoothed (40 ms attack, 100 ms release), capped, a
  little different per syllable and shut in pauses; the NPC blinks while the scene is open and nods
  its head on stressed peaks (`LF_DialogueMouth`). Players within `m_fHearRange` (12 m) hear it too.
- Converted: the town clerk (`QuestGiver_Clerk.et`), voice generated with ElevenLabs.
  Not converted: witness, bank teller, shop clerks.
- Tests: `dialogue/flow` and `dialogue/mouth` (LOGIC), `dialogue/clerk` (WORLD); `dialogue-pipeline-selftest.mjs`.
  Session proof: `node agent-box/session.mjs dialogue-proof`; harness verb `dialogue`.
- Not verified by automation: how the voice sounds and its level in the mix; number keys and a
  real pad. Findings and limits: `docs/architecture/npc-dialogue.md`.

### `LF_PhotoIdCard` — `Feature/Identity/LF_PhotoIdCard.c` (#902, 2026-11-13)
- ✅ The identity card is a photo card: Inspect it in the inventory and a laminated card sheet
  opens (UI kit `PORTRAIT` part): the holder's head and shoulders drawn client-side from their
  look at the date of issue (`LF_WantedPortrait`), surname, given names, age, date of issue
  (in-game), card number (`EV 000042`) and the civil registry stamp. Show ID opens the same
  sheet on the other player's screen for `idCard.showSeconds` (8 s). An on-duty officer has
  *Request ID* in the interaction menu (`request_id` slot): the citizen is asked on an ID CHECK
  sheet and shows the card or refuses (30 s window); reading a wanted player's own valid card
  identifies them, as before.
- ✅ Every printed card is a registry row (number, holder, name, age, date, look), the `idcards`
  payload (save version 30); the item carries only its number (`LF_SerialComponent`, which
  persists with it anywhere) and is printed again from the row after a restart.
- ✅ Lost or stolen: spawning hands out no new card. Talking to the town clerk, *I have lost my
  identity card.* (offered while you were issued one and do not carry it) prints a new card
  with a new number, a new photo and today's date for `idCard.replacementFee` (50, carried
  cash, ledger reason `id-card`); the old card reads VOID - REPLACED. A mode with
  `m_bGrantIdAtSpawn` replaces it free at the next spawn.
- Tests: `identity/photo-id-card` (LOGIC), `identity/photo-id-card-world` (WORLD),
  `persist/photo-id-cards` (PERSISTENCE), `e2e/pass2-verify-load` (the card in the pocket);
  session proof `photo-id-card-proof`. Logs `[LFDebug:IdCard]`.

### `LF_AssetAnim` — `Feature/AssetAnim/` (#340, 2026-11-14)

The component block, the push clips (`Anims/LF/Drill/*.txa`, solved from `tools/assets/drill/clips/*.json`), the workspace `LF_DrillPush` and the sparks effect are generated by `tools\cli asset anim drill` from `tools/assets/drill/spec.json` and checked by `asset-anim-selftest`. Tests: `assetanim/asset-anim` (WORLD); proof `session.mjs drill-scene-proof` (late join, both clients, filmed with sound). ### `LF_NpcStun` — `Feature/Police/LF_NpcStun.c`, `Core/Npcs/LF_NpcBaseEntity.c` (#922, 2026-11-14)
- ✅ **The taser stuns town NPCs.** A town NPC is an `LF_NpcBaseEntity` with no damage manager, so a dart that hits one reports to nobody (the clerk never went down). The shot is read from the shooter instead: the server's `OnProjectileShot` handler on every player body (`LF_EvidenceShooter`) calls `LF_NpcStun.OnShot`, which, when the held weapon carries `LF_StunWeaponComponent`, follows the aim line for the weapon's reach and stuns the nearest NPC within 0.6 m of it (`PickTarget`, the pure rule) with a clear line (`Blocked`). The stunned NPC falls onto its back (`LF_NpcBaseEntity`: replicated `m_bLF_Stunned` plus rest origin and angles, each peer poses it from them, so a late joiner sees it), the shooter gets the toast `#LF-Police_NpcStunned`, a talk, shop or witness question is refused (`LF_NpcStun.BlocksTalk` in `LF_Dialogue`, `LF_QuestAction`, `LF_ShopAction`, `LF_WitnessAskAction`; an open conversation ends), and the server stands it up after the weapon's `m_fStunSeconds` (token-guarded). A dying NPC or one already down is not stunned. Guns still do not hurt protected NPCs. Tests `police/npc-stun` (pick), `police/npc-stun-world` (pose, talk block, wake, stale wake); harness `shootnpc <name>` with the taser in hand runs the same code, `seen` reports `npc=<name>:stun=S:up=Y`; session proof `npc-stun-proof`.
- ⚠️ The shot direction on a real trigger pull (aiming component, else the projectile's heading) is untested: the harness cannot pull a trigger. A civilian with a hit zone also takes the dart's 1 damage. The stun is not persisted. No buzz sound on an NPC.
- ✅ (2026-11-14, #921) The player HUD root layout (`UI/Layouts/HUD/HUDLayouts/EL_HUDManagerRoot.layout`) has the vanilla `Slot_Vehicle`, `Slot_WeaponInfo` and `Slot_AvailableActions` slots again, so the vehicle dashboard indicators (lights, high beams) draw bottom-left instead of mid-screen. `validate-hud-root-slots` keeps them there. Harness `input` accepts `VehicleLightsToggle` and `CarLightsHiBeamToggle`; proof verb `vehicle-hud-proof`.
- ✅ **Cash is always the money mesh** (#932). `MoneyStack.et` no longer swaps to a backpack mesh at 1000 and 10000; the playtest top-up carries 20000, so every cash item in the inventory showed as a field pack. `LF_Test_QuantityOperations` pins it. No save change.
