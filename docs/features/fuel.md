### `LF_FuelStockRules` — `Feature/Fuel/LF_FuelStockRules.c` (#3327, framework #788)
- ✅ Pure station-stock rules, no world (merged, in-dev). A station (`LF_FuelStock`) holds litres up to a capacity. `PlanSale` clamps the litres by stock first, then by what the buyer can pay, and charges only for the litres that move; an empty station (under the minimum stock to sell) refuses with EMPTY, cash that cannot buy one litre with NO_MONEY, price 0 is the kill switch (OFF). `PlanDelivery` lands only the room left and pays only for the litres that landed; a full station refuses with FULL. Prices are hundredths of a cash unit per litre; the fractional part rides in `LF_FuelChargeLedger` (per key, unsaved by design), so ten litres at 0.30 cost exactly 3. Plans do not change the station; `ApplySale` / `ApplyDelivery` do. Int maths through `LF_Utils`. Tests: `fuel/fuel-stock-rules` (LOGIC).

### `LF_FuelStationComponent`, `LF_FuelBuyAction` — `Feature/Fuel/LF_FuelStationComponent.c`, `LF_FuelBuyAction.c`, `Prefabs/Props/Fuel/LF_FuelPump.et` (#3328, framework #788)
- 🔧 A pump that sells from its station's stock (merged, in-dev). Put `LF_FuelStationComponent` (station id attribute) on a pump with `LF_FuelBuyAction`; `Prefabs/Props/Fuel/LF_FuelPump.et` is the vanilla tank pistol with both. While the fuel config is on, "Fill the tank" finds the vehicle at the pump (server, 6 m) and `Sell` works out everything itself: litres = the tank's free room cut by the station's stock (`LF_FuelStock`, one per station id, shared by its pumps, made from the config row) and by the buyer's cash (`LF_FuelStockRules.PlanSaleFor`); it takes the cash, fills the tank, then takes the litres from the station. A client sends no litres and no price. An empty station refuses with "The tanks are dry. The depot truck is due."; no cash, a full tank and no vehicle each have a line; a dead, downed or cuffed buyer is refused; a refusal changes nothing. The vanilla free refuel is off at that pump (`SCR_FuelSupportStationComponent.IsValid` is modded), only while the config is on; the core ships it off, so a pump behaves as vanilla until a server opts in. Not yet: the pack's pumps still use the vanilla prefab with no station id, so no pump in the world sells yet: a later card. The stock is saved (next entry). Test `fuel/station-stock` (WORLD).
### `LF_FuelStockSavePayload` — `Feature/Fuel/LF_FuelStockSave.c`, save section `fuelstock` (#3329, framework #788)
- 🔧 Each station's litres survive a restart (merged, in-dev). A save section (v1) of `LF_FuelStockRow` (station id, litres), one per station opened so far, sorted by id; the capacity is never saved, the config owns it. A load replaces every stock: litres cut to the capacity, an empty id, a negative count and a duplicate id dropped with a log line, and (when the config lists its stations) an id with no config row dropped as a retired station; a config with no rows keeps any id (table values). A save from before the section has nothing to load (a station opens at its starting litres on first use, and an unrelated store load leaves open stations alone). A dry station is still dry after a restart and a delivery is still there. `LF_FuelStationComponent` gained `Stocks()` and `ForgetStocks()` for it. ### The tanker haul — `Feature/Fuel/LF_FuelHaul.c`, `LF_FuelDepotComponent.c`, `Prefabs/Props/Fuel/LF_FuelDepot.et`, grid cell `fuel-haul` (#3330, framework #788)
- 🔧 Buy a load at the depot, pump it into a station, get paid for what arrived (merged, in-dev). `LF_FuelDepotComponent` plus `LF_FuelDepotLoadAction` ("Load the tanker") on `LF_FuelDepot.et`: the server finds the tanker within 12 m (a vehicle with a fuel-storage node, the vanilla Ural tanker's cistern, never its engine tank) and `LF_FuelHaul.Buy` loads the configured litres per delivery, cut by the cargo tank's room and the hauler's cash, charged at `m_iWholesalePerLitre` for the litres that went in (cash first, then the tank; a refusal changes nothing; no cash at all buys nothing). `LF_FuelHaulPumpAction` ("Pump into the station tanks", added to `LF_FuelPump.et`) calls `LF_FuelHaul.PumpIn`: only the station's free room lands (`PlanDelivery`), the litres are taken out of the tanker first, the hauler is paid `m_iDeliveryPayPerLitre` for the litres that landed and the rest stays in the tanker; a full station (STATION_FULL) and an empty tanker refuse. Only litres bought at a depot are paid: `LF_FuelHaul` keeps the bought litres per tanker (unsaved, like vehicle fuel), and a pump-in offers the smaller of the cargo and that count, so the vanilla Ural's 900 l spawn fill and a free vanilla refill earn nothing (NOT_BOUGHT, "That fuel was not bought at the depot."). The same checks as a pump sale: fuel stock on, a hauler alive, not downed or cuffed. A client sends no litres and no price; the stock is the saved per-station stock. Grid cell `fuel-haul` (c17r3, GridWorld) holds a depot and a pump (station id `grid-haul`). Test `fuel/haul` (WORLD): 500 l bought, 300 l of room: stock +300, paid for 300, 200 l left in the tanker. The Montignac depot kit (`tools/town/kits/fuel-depot.json`) has a `depot-load` row for it; the layer `Place_montignac_depot.layer` gets the barrel when a `town-` pass runs `town fit montignac depot --refit` (refused outside `ws/town-draft-montignac-depot`). The tanker comes from outside the cell (grid cells do not place vehicles).
