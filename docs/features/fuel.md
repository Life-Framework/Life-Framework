# Fuel

Design context: `addons/LifeFramework/Scripts/Game/Feature/Fuel/Feature.md`.

### `LF_FuelStockRules` — `Feature/Fuel/LF_FuelStockRules.c` (#3327, framework #788)
- ✅ Pure station-stock rules, no world (merged, in-dev). A station (`LF_FuelStock`) holds litres up to a capacity. `PlanSale` clamps the litres by stock first, then by what the buyer can pay, and charges only for the litres that move; an empty station (under the minimum stock to sell) refuses with EMPTY, cash that cannot buy one litre with NO_MONEY, price 0 is the kill switch (OFF). `PlanDelivery` lands only the room left and pays only for the litres that landed; a full station refuses with FULL. Prices are hundredths of a cash unit per litre; the fractional part rides in `LF_FuelChargeLedger` (per key, unsaved by design), so ten litres at 0.30 cost exactly 3. Plans do not change the station; `ApplySale` / `ApplyDelivery` do. Int maths through `LF_Utils`. Tests: `fuel/fuel-stock-rules` (LOGIC).

### The 1989 fuel numbers — `Configs/Fuel/LF_Fuel_Everon1989.conf` (pack, #3331)
- 🔧 Pack overlay of `LF_FuelConfig` (listed in the mission header; `LF_FuelConfig.ApplyLayers` reads it, an overlay sets only the fields it writes). New core fields: `m_iWholesalePerLitre` (the depot's price per litre, core 60), `m_TankerPrefab` (empty in the core), `m_aStations` (`LF_FuelStationRow`: id, capacity, starting litres; an overlay's list replaces the earlier one; `NewStationFor(id)` makes a stock from its row, else the table values). The pack turns fuel on, wholesale 0.90, delivery pay 1.35 a litre, a 1000 l tanker load (vanilla `Ural4320_tanker_CIV_orange.et`), stations `saint-philippe` 3000 l (900 at start), `saint-pierre` 2500 (700), `morton` 2000 (500). A full haul costs $900 at the depot, pays $1350, margin $450 (a third of the pay), about $450/h at an assumed hour a haul: above the garbage round's ~$400/h, 2x under police patrol pay; the only route job you can lose money on. Test `everon/fuel-haul` (pack leg).
