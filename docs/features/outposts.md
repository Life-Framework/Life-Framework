### `LF_Outpost` — `Feature/Outposts/LF_Outpost.c` (#4217, 2026-11-23)
- ⚠️ A rebel outpost is a safe zone: `LF_OutpostComponent` registers a SAFE circle (radius from `Configs/Outposts/LF_Outposts.conf`, 60 m by default) and the place under kind `outpost`. Nobody fights inside; police may walk in. A trader inside refuses an on-duty officer with a toast ("We don't deal with the law here. Take the badge off and come back."); off-duty police are served. ### `LF_OutpostStanding` — `Feature/Outposts/LF_OutpostStanding.c` (#4224, GA-O4, 2026-11-23)
- 🧪 A completed trade at a trader or shop till inside an outpost earns standing on the one underworld track (`LF_Reputation`, the `fence_sale` award row: 5 points, 120 s cooldown, the UNDERWORLD daily cap) and marks the character "known here" at that outpost (`LF_OutpostStanding.IsKnownHere(uid, outpostId)`). There is no per-outpost standing; the flag sits on top of the one track (N27). The flags are keyed by the character record key and the outpost id and saved in the `outpoststanding` section. The trader sale (`LF_TraderSale.Sell`, both the list and the premium-buyer path) and the shop till (`LF_CharacterShopComponent.ServeTrade`, buy and sell) now ask `LF_Outpost.RefusesService` first: an on-duty officer gets the refusal toast and keeps the goods (trader outcome `OUTPOST_REFUSED`; the till slip shows the clerk refusal). A trade outside every outpost changes nothing. Nothing reads the flag yet, and no outpost is placed. Test `outposts/standing-award` (PERSISTENCE, `wt e2e` reload check). Merged, in-dev.

The server decides every sale in `LF_OutpostStock.Buy`: an on-duty officer inside an outpost is refused first (`LF_Outpost.RefusesService` and `NotifyRefusal`, #4217), then the tier gate refuses in words ("I don't know you well enough for that one. ### `LF_OutpostBuyer` — `Feature/Outposts/LF_OutpostBuyer.c` (#4225, GA-O5, 2026-11-23)
- 🧪 The outpost's contraband buyer is the far end of a convoy run. It takes a contraband crate (`LF_ContrabandCrate`) for 10 to 20% more than the fence pays. The fence price is the convoy drop, `LF_Contraband.PAYOUT` ($200), so the built-in price is $230; `PriceAboveFence` holds the premium to 10..20%.
  - **One crate per run:** a sale closes the seller's running, driven run through `LF_Contraband.Deliver`, which marks it paid and starts the 10-minute cooldown. A crate with no run, an undriven run or a run already paid fetches nothing ("I only take crates that came up the road on a run.").
  - **Demand cap:** the buyer takes a crate only while its demand allows. Each outpost has one buyer-demand purse (`LF_BuyerDemand`, CR-B1) under buyer id `outpost:<id>`; the built-in row holds 6 crates and refills one per real hour. The purse is kept in `LF_BuyerDemandBook`, so the `buyerdemand` save section keeps it across a restart. A pack may set its own row in `LF_BuyerDemand.conf`: lot size 1, and a curve good is honored.
  - **At the cap:** the buyer refuses in words ("I have enough for now. Come back in about N minutes.") and the crate stays with the seller.
  - **Officers and standing:** an on-duty officer inside the outpost is refused (`LF_Outpost.RefusesService`, `NotifyRefusal`) and keeps the crates. A completed sale books the `fence_sale` standing and the known-here flag (`LF_OutpostStanding.OnSale`).
  - **Order:** cash first (extra cash drops at the seller's feet), then the crate, then the run is closed. Any failure puts the cash, the crate and the purse back.
  - **Status:** `LF_OutpostBuyerSellAction` ("Sell contraband crate ($230)") is the table's action row, but no buyer table is placed yet (O3, O9). Test `outposts/buyer-cap` (WORLD). Merged, in-dev.
