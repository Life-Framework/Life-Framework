### `LF_FishingConfig` — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingConfig.c`, `Configs/Fishing/LF_Fishing.conf` (#2927)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) The fishing data: rods (`m_Prefab`, `m_fCastRange`, `m_fBiteSpeed`, `m_iMinFisherLevel`), baits (`m_fBiteSpeed`, `m_aBoostSpecies`), species (`m_sId`, three size prefabs, `m_aWater` OCEAN or POND, `m_aHours`, `m_iMinFisherLevel`), tables (weighted species, three size weights) and the globals (bite 15 to 60 s, strike window 2 s, slack 0.5 s, 6 casts a minute, empty-spot slowdown, bait boost, the open-water table). `Sanitize` runs at load: a bad rod, bait, species or table is removed with `LF_Debug.Error("Fishing", ...)` naming it, a bad table entry is dropped alone, a bad global falls back to its default; no other row is touched. The core ships one neutral set (mackerel, bass, tuna, perch, carp; tables `open_water`, `harbour`, `pond`) with empty fish prefabs and placeholder rod and bait prefabs until the 3D pipeline (#339) and the pack (#800) supply them.

### `LF_FishingRules` — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingRules.c` (#2927)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) Pure server rules, every random draw passed in as a 0..1 roll. `BiteSeconds` (rod and bait speed, slower at an emptied spot, always inside min and max), `Strike` (EARLY, HIT, LATE: inside window plus slack lands), `InCastRange`, `RodUsable`, `TryCast` (casts per minute), `ResolveTable` (the spot's table while it has stock, else the open-water table), `PickSpecies` (weighted; water kind, hour and level filters, so a pond never gives an ocean fish; bait boost), `PickSize`, `PickCatch` (open water is always small; a spot table with nothing eligible falls back to open water). A spot's stock is the existing `LF_GatherStock`. Nothing here is wired to the world yet: the cast and strike RPCs, the spot component and the licence are later cards of the chapter. Test: `LF_Test_FishingRules` (LOGIC).
