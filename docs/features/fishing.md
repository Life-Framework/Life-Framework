# Fishing

Design context: `addons/LifeFramework/Scripts/Game/Feature/Fishing/Feature.md`, `docs/architecture/fishing.md`.

### `LF_FishingConfig` — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingConfig.c`, `Configs/Fishing/LF_Fishing.conf` (#2927)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) The fishing data: rods (`m_Prefab`, `m_fCastRange`, `m_fBiteSpeed`, `m_iMinFisherLevel`), baits (`m_fBiteSpeed`, `m_aBoostSpecies`), species (`m_sId`, three size prefabs, `m_aWater` OCEAN or POND, `m_aHours`, `m_iMinFisherLevel`), tables (weighted species, three size weights) and the globals (bite 15 to 60 s, strike window 2 s, slack 0.5 s, 6 casts a minute, empty-spot slowdown, bait boost, the open-water table). `Sanitize` runs at load: a bad rod, bait, species or table is removed with `LF_Debug.Error("Fishing", ...)` naming it, a bad table entry is dropped alone, a bad global falls back to its default; no other row is touched. The core ships one neutral set (mackerel, bass, tuna, perch, carp; tables `open_water`, `harbour`, `pond`) with empty fish prefabs and placeholder rod and bait prefabs until the 3D pipeline (#339) and the pack (#800) supply them.

### `LF_FishingRules` — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingRules.c` (#2927)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) Pure server rules, every random draw passed in as a 0..1 roll. `BiteSeconds` (rod and bait speed, slower at an emptied spot, always inside min and max), `Strike` (EARLY, HIT, LATE: inside window plus slack lands), `InCastRange`, `RodUsable`, `TryCast` (casts per minute), `ResolveTable` (the spot's table while it has stock, else the open-water table), `PickSpecies` (weighted; water kind, hour and level filters, so a pond never gives an ocean fish; bait boost), `PickSize`, `PickCatch` (open water is always small; a spot table with nothing eligible falls back to open water). A spot's stock is the existing `LF_GatherStock`. Nothing here is wired to the world yet: the cast and strike RPCs, the spot component and the licence are later cards of the chapter. Test: `LF_Test_FishingRules` (LOGIC).

### Fishing items — `Prefabs/Items/Fishing/`, `Assets/Items/Fishing/` (#2928)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #2928) The first fishing items, each with its own Blender-modelled mesh and baked material (`tools/assets/fishing-rod|bait-tin|fish-trout*`, shared builders in `tools/assets/fishing-lib/geo.py`; no generation credits). `LF_FishingRod.et` is a core hand tool (`LF_HandToolComponent`, tool id `rod`, the drill's wrench hold pose; a cast animation waits for #340), 1.2 m, 3 l. `LF_BaitTin.et` is a round green tin, 0.3 l. The trout is one species in three prefabs on one base: `LF_FishTrout.et` (the medium, 0.30 m, owns the components) with `LF_FishTroutSmall.et` (0.18 m) and `LF_FishTroutLarge.et` (0.44 m) overriding only the mesh, volume and weight (the same model, scaled by the clean stage, because a prefab `scale` is lost when an item leaves an inventory). The config (`LF_Fishing.conf`) still points rod and bait at the #2927 placeholders (`Prefabs/Tools/Pickaxe.et`, `Prefabs/Items/Food/Apple.et`); #2929 swaps in these prefabs. Test `fishing/items`.

### Fishing spots and the counters — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingSpotComponent.c`, `Prefabs/Systems/Fishing/LF_FishingSpot.et`, `Configs/Catalogue/LF_ItemCatalogue.conf` (#2929)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #2929) A map maker drops `LF_FishingSpot.et` (table, radius, stock, regrow seconds, optional id and map state) and it registers in `LF_Places` as kind `FishingSpot` on every machine, so the cast finds it by kind and never by name or coordinate. `LF_FishingSpotComponent.FindSpotFor(target)` returns the spot whose radius covers the target (flat distance, the nearer marker when two overlap) or null for open water; the server-side stock is the existing `LF_GatherStock` (one fewer per catch, regrows, never saved). A bad marker (no table, radius or stock) logs `LF_Debug.Error("Fishing", ...)` and is not placed. The core catalogue (`LF_ItemCatalogue.conf`) now puts the three trout sizes on the `fish_counter` outlet (the counter pays 6, 12 and 25, under what it asks) and the rod and bait tin on the `chandler` (and the tin on the counter). The fishing config now names the real rod and bait tin instead of the #2927 placeholders. No cast or strike RPC yet: the spot is data and lookup until the cast card. Test `fishing/spots`.
