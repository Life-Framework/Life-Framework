### Cast, bite and strike — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingSession.c`, `LF_FishingRodComponent.c`, `Prefabs/Items/Fishing/LF_FishingRod.et` (#2930)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #2930, draft for review) The server half of the cast. With the rod in the hands (`LF_FishingRodComponent`, the rod's hand tool, now listening for the fire input on the owner's machine) the owner asks `LF_AskFishingCast(target)` on the PlayerController; the server (`LF_FishingSession.CastAt`) refuses with a toast and a `[LFDebug:Fishing]` line unless the angler is alive and on foot, holds the rod (and the rod is in the config), has the FISHER level, is under the casts-a-minute limit, has no cast out, is within the rod's range, the target is OCEAN or POND water (`TryGetWaterSurface` at the terrain height the server reads itself) and a bait item is in the inventory. A cast stores one open cast per player (session state, never saved) and starts one `CallLater` for the bite (15 to 60 s, scaled by rod, bait and an emptied spot). At the bite the owner gets "Strike!" (toast plus `LF_FishingEvent` to the owner for the float). A strike (`LF_AskFishingStrike(castId)`) is ignored unless the server's own bite has happened and the cast id matches; inside the window plus slack it rolls the catch from the spot's table (open water: small fish), gives one fish (dropped beside the angler when the pack is full), spends one bait item, thins the spot's stock and awards `fish_catch` XP; after the window the bait is gone and nothing is given; a bite nobody strikes ends the same way. Walking more than 5 m, putting the rod away, dying or boarding a vehicle ends a cast with nothing spent. Test `fishing/cast`. Not built yet: the float prop and its dip sound, the `fish_catch` row of the FISHER track, the `LF_OnFishCaught` bus event, the grid pond cell and the session pictures.
