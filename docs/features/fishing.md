### `LF_FishingRules` — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingRules.c` (#2927)
- ✅ (2026-11-18, merged, in-dev) Pure server rules, every random draw passed in as a 0..1 roll. `BiteSeconds` (rod and bait speed, slower at an emptied spot, always inside min and max), `Strike` (EARLY, HIT, LATE: inside window plus slack lands), `InCastRange`, `RodUsable`, `TryCast` (casts per minute), `ResolveTable` (the spot's table while it has stock, else the open-water table), `PickSpecies` (weighted; water kind, hour and level filters, so a pond never gives an ocean fish; bait boost), `PickSize`, `PickCatch` (open water is always small; a spot table with nothing eligible falls back to open water). A spot's stock is the existing `LF_GatherStock`. Nothing here is wired to the world yet: the cast and strike RPCs, the spot component and the licence are later cards of the chapter. Test: `LF_Test_FishingRules` (LOGIC).

### Cast, bite and strike — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingSession.c`, `LF_FishingRodComponent.c`, `Prefabs/Items/Fishing/LF_FishingRod.et` (#2930)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #2930, draft for review) The server half of the cast. With the rod in the hands (`LF_FishingRodComponent`, the rod's hand tool, now listening for the fire input on the owner's machine) the owner asks `LF_AskFishingCast(target)` on the PlayerController; the server (`LF_FishingSession.CastAt`) refuses with a toast and a `[LFDebug:Fishing]` line unless the angler is alive and on foot, holds the rod (and the rod is in the config), has the FISHER level, is under the casts-a-minute limit, has no cast out, is within the rod's range, the target is OCEAN or POND water (`TryGetWaterSurface` at the terrain height the server reads itself) and a bait item is in the inventory. A cast stores one open cast per player (session state, never saved) and starts one `CallLater` for the bite (15 to 60 s, scaled by rod, bait and an emptied spot). At the bite the owner gets "Strike!" (toast plus `LF_FishingEvent` to the owner for the float). A strike (`LF_AskFishingStrike(castId)`) is ignored unless the server's own bite has happened and the cast id matches; inside the window plus slack it rolls the catch from the spot's table (open water: small fish), gives one fish (dropped beside the angler when the pack is full), spends one bait item, thins the spot's stock and awards `fish_catch` XP; after the window the bait is gone and nothing is given; a bite nobody strikes ends the same way. Walking more than 5 m, putting the rod away, dying or boarding a vehicle ends a cast with nothing spent. Test `fishing/cast`. Not built yet: the float prop and its dip sound, the `fish_catch` row of the FISHER track, the `LF_OnFishCaught` bus event, the grid pond cell and the session pictures.

### The float, the grid pond and the catch XP — `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingFloat.c`, `LF_FishingEvents.c`, `Prefabs/Systems/Fishing/LF_FishingFloat.et`, `GridWorld_Layers/Grid_slice_fi4.layer` (#2995)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #2995) The owner's half of the cast. A float (a red-and-white Blender bobber, `Assets/Items/Fishing/FishingFloat`, shown at three times its size so it reads on a flat screen) is a local entity with no `RplComponent`, put on the water at the cast point when the server says the line is out (`LF_FishingRodComponent.OnServerEvent` CAST_OK), pulled under and bobbing with the base game's UI click when the bite arrives (BITE), and taken away on a catch, a miss, an ended cast or the rod leaving the hands. Nothing per cast is broadcast and the dedicated server never draws one. The cast aims at the first water along the view (3 m out to the rod's reach; open ground is still a request the server refuses as not water). A landed fish now pays the FISHER `fish_catch` XP (the `FISHER` track and the `fish_catch` row, 10 XP, daily cap 2000, are in `Configs/Jobs/LF_Jobs.conf`; the cast already checked the FISHER level, which read 1 before the track existed) and publishes `LF_OnFishCaught` (player record key, species id, size 0/1/2, fish prefab, water kind, spot table) on the server bus after the stock is thinned. The core ships one `trout` species with the three trout prefabs in the `pond` table so a core server's pond gives a fish the fish counter buys; the pack overlays the 1989 species. Core props `LF_FishCounter.et` (outlet `fish_counter`) and `LF_Chandler.et` (outlet `chandler`). The proof ground is the grid cell `slice-fi4`: a real pond (a base-game `LakeGeneratorEntity`, `TryGetWaterSurface` answers POND over it), a `LF_FishingSpot` over it (table `pond`), the chandler and the fish counter. Tests `fishing/float` and `fishing/cast` (FISHER XP and the event); session proof `agent-box/proofs/fishing-proof.mjs` with the harness verb `fishing fire|auto|state|water` (buy rod and bait, cast, the float dips, strike, a trout and XP, sell it for cash). Not built yet: a splash sound (the cue is the UI click until the sound pipeline), the licence gate on the fish counter, the pack's species and prices, the smokehouse.

### Every species lands a fish — `Prefabs/Items/Fishing/LF_Fish{Mackerel,Bass,Tuna,Perch,Carp}*.et`, `Feature/Fishing/LF_FishingConfig.c` (#3119)
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #3119) Mackerel, bass, tuna, perch and carp had empty size prefabs, so every harbour and open-water strike came back `EMPTY` and spent the bait; only pond trout worked. Each species now has small, medium and large prefabs (`LF_Fish<Species>[Small|Large].et`), children of the three trout prefabs (same model, own neutral names `#LF-Item_Fish<Species>_*`), with catalogue rows and `fish_counter` offers. The counter pays small/medium/large: mackerel and perch 5/10/20, carp 7/14/28, bass 8/16/32, tuna 15/30/60 (trout stays 6/12/25); a pack may rename them or give each its own model. `Sanitize` now drops a table entry whose species has an empty size prefab, with `LF_Debug.Error("Fishing", ...)` naming the species and size, so a bad row costs that fish, not the bait. `ShopPrices.conf` hides the fish from the general shop (its default buy price 50 sat under the large tuna, a profit loop); only the fish counter trades them. Test `fishing/species-prefabs`.
- 🧪 (2026-11-18, #3134) Every species has its own model: mackerel, bass, tuna, perch and carp no longer reuse the trout mesh. One Meshy text-to-3D generation per species (meshy-6-lite preview, refine, remesh to 4000 triangles; 75 + 25 credits; no CC0 or CC-BY fish fit), cleaned and scaled by the asset pipeline into three sizes each (`tools/assets/fish-<species>[-small|-large]/`, `Assets/Items/Fishing/Fish<Species>[Small|Large]/`, the small and large specs reuse the medium's Meshy task ids and cost nothing). Each size prefab keeps its GUID and overrides only the `MeshObject` and the inventory preview distance (3 x the fish length, so the tuna fits the frame). Test `fishing/species-prefabs` (loads every size prefab).
