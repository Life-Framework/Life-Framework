# Law book

- ✅ Law-book codes (#4202): every `LF_CrimeEntry` in the crime catalog (`Feature/Crime/LF_CrimeCatalog.c`) carries a law-book section (`m_eLawSection`: criminal, road, narcotics, weapons), a stacking group (`m_sStackGroup`) and a jail-only flag (`m_bJailOnly`). New and unchanged rows default to criminal, no group, finable, so no save or shipped `.conf` changes. The owner's `crime` section sets them per code with `lawSection`, `stackGroup` and `jailOnly`; a bad section is refused and logged `[LFDebug:Law]`. `LF_LawCodeRules` (`Feature/Law/`) totals a charge list by rule: two codes in one group stack to the higher, codes in different groups (or with no group) add, a jail-only code refuses a fine but its time still counts. Server logic only, no UI yet; the pack's codes come with L2 and the charge sheet with L3. Test `law/code-stacking`.
- ✅ Charge sheet rules (#4209): `LF_ChargeSheetRules.Evaluate` (`Feature/Law/LF_ChargeSheetRules.c`) turns the ticked codes into an `LF_ChargeSheetResult`. The fine stacks by the law book, a code the suspect was already charged with today counts double (fine and time, before the stacking comparison), and the total is clamped to the officer's rank fine cap (pack: $500 patrol to $5,000 lieutenant). A jail-only code on the sheet removes "pay now". Time adds up to 60 minutes. Any on-duty officer signs; an off-duty officer or an empty sheet is refused, and a code ticked twice counts once. The `sergeant` ladder row and above (the top rank on a ladder without one) may switch a sheet to jail only: the fine drops to 0, the time stays, and `[LFDebug:Law]` logs it; a lower rank, or a sheet with no time, is refused and logged. Pure server rules, no money moves and no RPC; the booking desk (L5) calls it. Test `law/charge-sheet-rules`.

- ✅ Booking desk and the offer (#4420, police L5): `LF_BookingDesk.et` (`Prefabs/Props/Police/`, a vanilla short desk) carries **Book suspect** for an on-duty officer. The server picks the cuffed suspect beside the officer and `LF_BookingDesk.RequestBook` re-checks it (the id must resolve to a cuffed character within arrest reach, not the officer), then builds the charge sheet from the suspect's open crime reports through `LF_ChargeSheetRules` with the officer's rank and duty from the server's own account. The offer goes to the suspect's machine only (an owner RPC on their body) and shows them two scroll actions on the desk: **Pay $X now** (only when the sheet offers a fine) and **Take the time (N min)**. Taking the time books them through `LF_JailBooking.Book` with the sheet's share of the jail's own length (#4207 cap). Paying goes through `LF_ChargePayment` (#4427). One open offer per suspect for five minutes; a second booking, an off-duty officer or one more than 3 m from the desk is refused in words, and a desk more than 15 m from the jail cell opens no offer (place it in the cell block). An offer is dropped once the suspect is jailed another way or is no longer wanted and logged `[LFDebug:Law]`. The station placement is L10. Test `law/booking-desk-offer`.
- ✅ Pay now (#4427, police L6): `Law/LF_ChargePayment.c`. **Pay $X now** at the booking desk takes the offer's fine (the server's number, never a client's) from carried cash first and the bank for the rest, all or nothing; a wallet that gives back less is put back and logged. The money goes to the town treasury: there is no treasury store yet, so it is a sink logged `[LFDebug:Law] treasury +$X`, and the booking officer's cash and bank never change. A paid suspect's wanted level drops to 0 (reports and posters come down), the cuffs come off and go back to the booking officer, and the spent offer pays nothing on a second click. A suspect whose cash and bank fall short goes to the cell for the offer's time (`LF_JailBooking.Book` when the booking officer is at hand, else `LF_Jail.Arrest` on the officer's booking). A refused fine-only charge costs a minute in the cell per $60 of fine, at least one, at most the jail's 15-minute cap (`LF_ChargePayment.RefusedFineSeconds`, used by the desk's offer). Test `law/charge-payment`.
