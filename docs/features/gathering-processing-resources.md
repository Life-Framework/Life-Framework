# Gathering / Processing / Resources

> **In short.** Mining, smelting and lumber are three working chains on Everon. For iron, take the miner job, swing a pickaxe at an iron rock for nuggets, add a lump of coal from a coal rock, and run both through the smelter: two nuggets and one coal make an iron bar, and bars sell at the dock wholesale counter. Coal also has its own buyer, a scale house by the power plant that pays per lump until its purse runs out for the hour. For lumber, buy an axe, fell a real tree (it falls away from you and its trunk becomes logs), saw the logs into planks at the sawmill, and sell the planks to a timber buyer. Tools wear, stock at a spot regrows, and levels unlock faster runs. All of this is built and in the current build; it is still being playtested, and the numbers may change. The rest of this page is the engineering record.
