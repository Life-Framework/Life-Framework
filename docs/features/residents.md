### `LF_ResidentMemory` - `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentMemory.c`, `LF_ResidentMemoryConfig.c`, `Configs/Residents/LF_ResidentMemory.conf` (#2859, 2026-11-18)
- How each resident feels about each character, as pure server rules keyed `residentId|uid`: `helped` raises, `crime_seen` lowers, clamped -100..100, no decay. Callers read a word (`refuses`, `wary`, `neutral`, `friendly`, `regular`) from config thresholds, never the number. A per-pair cooldown stops the same reason twice; a crime seen is gossiped one hop to the residents the witness knows (`m_aKnows`) at the config share, rounded toward zero. An unknown reason is refused with a `Residents` warning. Tests: `residents/resident-memory` (LOGIC).
- Resident memory survives a restart (#2861): the `residentmemory` save section holds one row per resident and character uid with the score and each reason's last award time (unix seconds, so a cooldown running at the save still runs after it). A neutral row (score 0, no cooldown running) is not written. A row for a resident the residents config no longer lists is kept, since a pack may bring the resident back, and logged once per resident per load. A save from before the section leaves memory as the session holds it (empty on a boot). The admin character wipe (`LF_Progression.Forget`) makes every resident forget that character. Tests: `residents/memory-persist` (PERSISTENCE, restart check in `wt e2e`).

### `LF_ResidentActivity` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentActivity.c` (#2907, 2026-11-18)
- A spawned resident does what their timetable line says instead of standing like a mannequin; the spawner calls `LF_ResidentActivity.Apply(entity, activity, spot)` once per spawn. The body is first turned to the anchor's facing. `sit`: a free `LF_SitAction` seat within 2.5 m of the anchor takes them (`LF_NpcLoiter.Sit`, the seat and pose a player's Sit uses, so late joiners see it through the base game loiter replication; the resident prefab must be a real character, `SCR_ChimeraCharacter`: a static `LF_NpcBaseEntity` body has no controller, so a `sit` or `loiter` for it stands with one warning); `serve`: they face the anchor's facing and stand behind the counter; `loiter`: the `LF_NpcLoiter` lean or smoke idle, picked by the resident's id; `idle` and empty: they face the anchor's facing and stand. An unknown activity idles with one `LF_Debug.Warn("Residents", ...)` per name; `sit` with no seat near the anchor idles with one warning. Server side, nothing persisted; a despawn frees the seat through `LF_NpcLoiter`'s watch. `LF_ResidentActivityComponent` does the same for a character a map maker places (`m_sActivity`), three seconds after the world loads. Proof: grid cell `slice-r1` (`tools\cli look grid:slice-r1`). Tests: `residents/activity` (WORLD).

### `LF_ResidentWalk` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentWalk.c` (#2909, 2026-11-18)
- A live resident whose timetable line moves to another place or anchor no longer pops. The spawner asks `LF_ResidentWalk.Move` first: with a player within 50 m of the resident, a new spot within 120 m and fewer than `m_iMaxWalking` (4, conf) walking, the resident gets up from their seat, their AI is switched on for the walk (one vanilla `Group_Base` with one `AIWaypoint_Move`, completion radius 1.5 m, group character speed capped to `EMovementType.WALK`) and they walk; on arrival the AI is switched off and the new line's activity starts. With nobody near (or too far, cap reached, or a world with no AIWorld) but a player within the despawn radius of the new spot, the body is moved there directly; with nobody near either end the spawner despawns them as before. A walk not arrived after `distance / 0.9 m/s + 20 s` moves the body onto the spot and logs `LF_Debug.Warn("Residents", ...)`. Server only, nothing persisted; walkers are ordinary replicated characters, so a late joiner sees them.
- **Limit:** the AI needs an AIWorld. GridWorld and Everon1989Town have none (`docs/features/events.md`), so there residents are placed, not walked; CoreOnEveron and MainWorld (the island) have one. On the island no resident moves yet for a different reason (#2996): every timetable line with a `House`, `CafeTable`, `FuelTill` or `StoreCounter` place is dropped at load (`LF_Places` knows no such place there), so the one line left, the teller at the bank counter, never changes anchor. The tests use a stand-in mover; the real AI walk is a manual step (below), and the grid proof cell moves to a follow-up card.
- **Tests:** `residents/walk` (WORLD).

### `LF_ResidentShopTerms` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentShopTerms.c`, `LF_ShopRefusal.RESIDENT_REFUSES` (#2911, 2026-11-18)
- When the clerk of a shop is a live resident (on a `work` line at the `counter` anchor, within 5 m of the shop entity), the clerk's memory word for the buyer sets the terms: `regular` pays `m_iRegularDiscountPercent` (default 10) less per unit, `refuses` is turned away with "Not to you." The price list shows base prices; the till slip shows what was charged. The percent has no `.conf` yet (field default). Tests: `residents/shop-terms` (WORLD).

### `LF_ResidentDirector` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentDirector.c` (#2910, 2026-11-18)
- With more residents due than free live slots (`m_iMaxLive` less the live count), the spawner no longer takes the first by id: it hands `LF_ResidentDirector.Pick` the due residents (each with its timetable line and resolved anchor spot), the player positions and the free slots, and spawns the ids it returns, best first. A counter worker (a `work` line on the `counter` anchor) within 15 m of a player ranks first; everyone else ranks by horizontal distance to the nearest player; ties go to the lower id. The place a player stands in is filled before one they cannot see. A resident the cap still holds back is logged once (`LF_Debug.Warn("Residents", ...)`) as before. Pure rules: no entity, world or state; the spawner calls it from one place in `SweepAt`. Server only, nothing persisted. Tests: `residents/resident-director` (LOGIC).
