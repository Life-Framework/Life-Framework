### `LF_ResidentMemory` - `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentMemory.c`, `LF_ResidentMemoryConfig.c`, `Configs/Residents/LF_ResidentMemory.conf` (#2859, 2026-11-18)

- Resident memory survives a restart (#2861): the `residentmemory` save section holds one row per resident and character uid with the score and each reason's last award time (unix seconds, so a cooldown running at the save still runs after it). A row for a resident the residents config no longer lists is kept, since a pack may bring the resident back, and logged once per resident per load. A save from before the section leaves memory as the session holds it (empty on a boot). The admin character wipe (`LF_Progression.Forget`) makes every resident forget that character. Tests: `residents/memory-persist` (PERSISTENCE, restart check in `wt e2e`).

### `LF_ResidentActivity` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentActivity.c` (#2907, 2026-11-18)

The body is first turned to the anchor's facing. `sit`: a free `LF_SitAction` seat within 2.5 m of the anchor takes them (`LF_NpcLoiter.Sit`, the seat and pose a player's Sit uses, so late joiners see it through the base game loiter replication; the resident prefab must be a real character, `SCR_ChimeraCharacter`: a static `LF_NpcBaseEntity` body has no controller, so a `sit` or `loiter` for it stands with one warning); `serve`: they face the anchor's facing and stand behind the counter; `loiter`: the `LF_NpcLoiter` lean or smoke idle, picked by the resident's id; `idle` and empty: they face the anchor's facing and stand. Server side, nothing persisted; a despawn frees the seat through `LF_NpcLoiter`'s watch. `LF_ResidentActivityComponent` does the same for a character a map maker places (`m_sActivity`), three seconds after the world loads. Proof: grid cell `slice-r1` (`tools\cli look grid:slice-r1`). Tests: `residents/activity` (WORLD).

### `LF_ResidentWalk` — `Feature/Residents/LF_ResidentWalk.c` (#2909, 2026-11-18)
- A live resident whose timetable line moves to another place or anchor no longer pops. The spawner asks `LF_ResidentWalk.Move` first: with a player within 50 m of the resident, a new spot within 120 m and fewer than `m_iMaxWalking` (4, conf) walking, the resident gets up from their seat, their AI is switched on for the walk (one vanilla `Group_Base` with one `AIWaypoint_Move`, completion radius 1.5 m, group character speed capped to `EMovementType.WALK`) and they walk; on arrival the AI is switched off and the new line's activity starts.
