## "None in reach": does not reproduce, and it is not a pickup fault

The 11-27 pay audit saw `truck-haul-proof` and `mining-iron-chain` fail with `none in reach` when picking up boards and ore. This run:
- Every board and ore pickup that the proofs make succeeded: `HardwoodBoard` 7 of 7 at **2.78 m**, `IronNugget` 10 of 10 (0.63 to 2.51 m), `CoalLump`, `IronBar` (2.0 m), all `via=SCR_PickUpItemAction`. `truck-haul-proof`, `mining-iron-chain` and `iron-chain-proof` all passed.
- The words `pickup ... none_in_reach filter=... reach=3` do appear in the logs (`LF_HarnessPickup.c:74`): they are the proof's own **end-of-loop probe** (it keeps pressing `pickup HardwoodBoard`, then `WoodPlank`, then `Firewood` until the answer is `none_in_reach`), counted under `verb-errors=` and not under the verdict. A run that read the last probe as the result would see "none in reach" with no real pickup failing; the audit doc does not say which it was.
- What can make a real pickup fail: the verb measures from the character's origin with a fixed reach of 3.0 m, and the boards fall 2.78 m away (0.22 m of margin) at the saw. A character standing a step further from the output than the proof does (a hand-written pay-audit loop, or after the truck is parked elsewhere) sees `none_in_reach` while the boards lie on the ground. The proof stands at the saw (`stand Fit_saint_philippe_woodlot_saw`) before it picks up (the 11-27 driving round added that), which is the likeliest reason it passes now; the pay-audit session of 11-26 ran before that change.
- Players pick up with the F key from the interaction distance, not from this probe, and `SCR_PickUpItemAction` accepted every item the harness reached. Nothing here stops a player from picking up their own boards or ore, so **no "RC blocker:" issue**.
