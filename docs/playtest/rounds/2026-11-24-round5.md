**Method limits.**
- The harness cannot walk or drive; travel is `stand <Entity> [side]` or a world capture. Sound was on and I cannot hear it. A pickaxe swing is
  `input CharacterMelee` with the aim set by `input CharacterTurnUp <deg>`; counts are by server log, not by swing.
- The booking desk was driven by the repo's own `booking-desk-proof` (steps read first); everything else by hand. `levelset MINER 10` skipped
  the daily cap.
- `resetplayer N` does not set cash to N: it tops up from a limited pool (`debug-topup`), so 3000 gave 3000 once and 300, 150 or 550 later.
  Cash for the Fine pickaxe came from the ATM (`use LF_ATMQuickAction__3` four times at `stand ...atms_drop_b79 e2`), and the Standard and
  Fine axes stayed unbought (row A10).
- `guide close` does not close a till sheet; `click CloseButton` ("Leave") does. A first-person capture depends on the aim, so several of my
  first pictures looked at the floor or the ceiling and were dropped; 41 pictures are kept in
  `website/public/docs-images/playtest-2026-11-24-r5/` (`img/` below), converted to JPEG before `session down`. I judged about 16 of them;
  the booking, handbook and F2 Person/Progress pictures are kept for the next reader and not graded (the F2 My file picture was read).
