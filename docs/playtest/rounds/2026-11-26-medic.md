A medic who reads the board, draws the ambulance and drives 300 m has little margin. The expiry is the board's `m_iBoardExpirySeconds`; a casualty that dies untreated should outlast the walk. 2. **Cadence: one roll per 1200 s with one medic**, so about three calls an hour at most, and only if each is taken within two minutes. Inside the 2-5 an hour band; the expiry above matters more than the interval.
