## Export orders are completable (flow 1 detail)

The 11-26 round could not finish the hand-in because the proof put the goods in the pockets and a pocket holds 3 to 4 planks. The hand-in counts the pockets plus the storage of a vehicle backed to the crane spot (`LF_ExportOrderCraneComponent.HandIn`). The Cargo truck's storage takes the whole of every order the pack opens. New pack test `everon/export-fits-truck` loads each template's full count into a spawned Cargo truck:

| Order | Count | Went into the truck |
|---|---|---|
| iron bars to the steamer | 40 | 40 |
| copper bars to the steamer | 20 | 20 |
| coal to the freighter | 100 | 100 |
| planks to the freighter | 120 | 120 |

So a typical order is physically completable and no order size or hand-in path was changed. What was missing was a way for a proof to load a truck: the harness verb `exportorder give truck` (dev gate) does the inventory-screen drag. Red proof: `--red` first ran hollow (doubling the counts still fits: the truck's storage holds 240 planks), then with the hook skipping the load `RED PROOF OK`; the green run passes (`fit check planks_freighter: 120 of 120`).
