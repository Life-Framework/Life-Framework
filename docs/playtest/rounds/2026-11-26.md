## Tooling notes (not player-facing)

1. `node agent-box/harness.mjs --client 1 send time 23` answers `time hour must be 0-24: undefined`; the verb needs `{ verb: "time", hour: 23 }` from a script (as `night-light-proof` does).
2. `registrar-proof` assumes a newcomer with $200 plus a $100 withdrawal pays the $250 founding fee, so it fails on any character with $0 to $199 cash (after another proof). It also reads the failed founding as a failed step with no hint about cash.
3. `export-crane-proof`'s `exportorder give short` puts count-1 of the order's goods in the pockets; order 4 wanted 120 planks, only 3 to 4 fit, the hand-in counts those and the final step fails. The proof was written for a small iron-bar order; it needs to pick an order or load a truck bed.
4. `outpost-north-proof` runs its buying rows at 12:00 by design; a night run of the buying rows needs `time 23` between the steps (the rows themselves were offered at night in the by-hand look).
