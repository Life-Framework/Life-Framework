## Top problems for a first-time player

1. **Nobody could join the hosted server** (`-noBackend` gives every player the NULL identity; the faction pick is refused and the player floats in the sky). Fixed in #159; proven with a join, a restart, and a rejoin.
2. **Police cannot arrest or escort on a dedicated server.** The offers never appear on a client. Fixed in #161; an arrest also needs a senior officer, which takes an hour on duty (#165).
3. **Every character is in underwear**, players and NPCs (#150). Police and civilians look identical; the first screenshot of the town is a naked man on a white plane.
4. **The registration form is unreadable** in a real client: dark sheet, dark text, "Editbox" labels, refusals invisible (#110, M1-13).
5. **Feedback is missing or misleading**: the parcel is invisible (#155), paid runs and sign-ups gave no toast on dedicated (fixed, #158), the car lot said "cannot afford" to a player holding the cash (fixed, #158), the contraband refusal is silent (#157).
6. **Money flows through menus the harness cannot open** (ATM, shop, org desk), so the middle of the journey (bank → car → org → hideout → garage) has never been seen working on a dedicated client (#108).
7. **Pacing**: one delivery is one job level; the first car is about 13 runs (#156).
