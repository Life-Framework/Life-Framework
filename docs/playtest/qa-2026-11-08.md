# Playtest QA, 2026-11-08

One pass through the M1 journey ([plan §a](../roadmap/m1-playtest-town.md#a-the-player-journey)) as a player, on the builds the maintainer will host. Bar: a stranger has a good time without help.

## Setup

- Build: `main` at `2c8420d`, before the M1-25 village merged (#137). Sites were on the old one-row street. Positions changed afterwards; the behaviour below did not.
- Dedicated server and two game clients: `node agent-box/session.mjs up --clients 2` (bare DebugWorld, dev gate open, `LF_SESSION_IDENTITY`). Driven with `harness --client n` (`ui`, `click`, `type`, `stand`, `look`, `use`, `press F`, `shot ui`) and `session.mjs state n`.
- Hosted playtest: `tools\cli host start` (mission `LF_PlaytestTown`, dev gate closed, `-noBackend`), one game client joined the way a friend does (`ArmaReforgerSteam.exe -client 127.0.0.1 -addonsDir <clone>\addons -addons LifeFramework`), then `host stop` and `host start` for the restart. Host dir was a scratch `LF_HOST_DIR`, not the maintainer's.
- Screenshots are game-client `shot ui` PNGs (640x360). Paths are under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\LifeFramework\session-qa\client<n>\logs\...\el-captures\`.

Status: **works**, **broken**, **confusing** (works, but a stranger would not know what happened), **ugly**, **not driven** (the harness could not reach it; see #108).

## Top problems for a first-time player

1. **Nobody could join the hosted server** (`-noBackend` gives every player the NULL identity; the faction pick is refused and the player floats in the sky). Fixed in this PR; proven with a join, a restart, and a rejoin.
2. **Police cannot arrest or escort on a dedicated server.** The offers never appear on a client. Fixed in the `qa-police-offers` PR.
3. **Every character is in underwear**, players and NPCs (#150). Police and civilians look identical; the first screenshot of the town is a naked man on a white plane.
4. **The registration form is unreadable** in a real client: dark sheet, dark text, "Editbox" labels, refusals invisible (#110, M1-13).
5. **Feedback is missing or misleading**: the parcel is invisible (#155), paid runs and sign-ups gave no toast on dedicated (fixed, `qa-feedback`), the car lot said "cannot afford" to a player holding the cash (fixed, `qa-feedback`), the contraband refusal is silent (#157).
6. **Money flows through menus the harness cannot open** (ATM, shop, org desk), so the middle of the journey (bank → car → org → hideout → garage) has never been seen working on a dedicated client (#108).
7. **Pacing**: one delivery is one job level; the first car is about 13 runs (#156).
