# Villages and rural sites sweep, 2026-11-27

Nine slots outside Saint-Philippe: Levie and Chotain pubs, the Meaux speed trap, the Montignac fuel depot, the still barn, the farm compound, the Everon sawmill, the chop yard and the hilltop mast. Each was shot by day with `tools\cli look place:<place>/<slot>` (the Levie pub also `--interior auto`), then all nine were walked at 22:00 in one full-profile island session (`agent-box/flows/island-villages-night.txt`, `agent-box/villages-night-shots.mjs`: the clock pinned to 22:00 with the harness `time` verb, `capture world` at eye height, the clock re-pinned every six shots). Method and bar as in `visual-sweep-2026-11-25.md` and `visual-sweep-night-2026-11-25.md`. Pictures are in `website/public/docs-images/sweep-villages-1127/`.

The look queue needed `--fresh` on this box: through the shared look server (`--serve`) two runs returned 70 of 70 shots missing because another worktree's request restarted the server mid-collect, so every picture here is from a private Workbench.

**Result: 2 OK, 7 BELOW, 7 fixed** (9 slots). All seven BELOW slots were the same defect at night: a place where the player does business with no light and no visible light source. Levie also had no sign. The day pass found nothing else: no floating, sunk or clipping props, no empty shelves and no debug text in any of the nine. Each fix is its own `town-draft-<place>-<slot>-night` PR; the audit (`audit placement --world MainWorld --baseline`, run over the seven layers together) is clean.

## Notes

- **Visible source.** Every new light is a lantern prop with an `LF_LanternGlow` on it (the north outpost recipe, #4978), or, in the pub and the still barn, the interior fill and chandelier recipe of the Saint-Philippe interiors. No light hangs without a prop except the meshless fills, which sit at lamp heads or over a desk.
- **Seating.** The first placement audit failed 13 rows: a drum is 1.085 m tall (the lanterns were 0.19 m sunk), the pallet box is a hollow frame (a lantern floated over it) and a flat pallet then failed its own contact check, and a hilltop crate has no surface. Those lanterns were re-seated or dropped; the sawmill lantern was put on the desk top by `place bounds` (the desk pivot is a corner).
- **Not fixed, by design.** Meaux and Chotain are OK. The pole-plate faces (depot sign, farm plate, sawmill sign) are runtime-painted and were not judged from a `look`; the night frames show the plates present and the farm plate lit.
- **Chop yard and sawmill rows were appended to the layers, not refitted:** both layers had drifted from their kits since the placement baseline pass, and a refit moves existing props. The Montignac kit now carries the tanker's vehicle lock that was hand-edited into its layer.
- **Method gaps.** The farm compound wide view and the Meaux patrol car did not stream in at night (camera under terrain / old position), so they carry no grade.
