# Night visual sweep, 2026-11-25

The first look at Saint-Philippe at night since the night lights (#4831: 31 window glows and 3 lamp heads; the ceiling lamps and the street poles come from the kits and the base map). One full-profile island session, clock pinned to 22:00 with the harness `time` verb, 42 stops, 176 clean world shots at eye height (the camera on the floor, the aim at 1.55 m for street and room frames, 2.4 m for fascias). The clock was re-pinned to 22:00 every six shots and the lights waited 35 s after the first set. Pictures are in `website/public/docs-images/night-sweep-1125/` (67 of the 176; the rest were reviewed and not kept). Nothing was fixed in this PR.

**Result: 25 OK, 17 BELOW** (42 stops).

**The street and the glows are good.** Every lamp pole and window glow that was checked lit, none hung in mid-air, and the main streets, the square, the bus stops and the lit shops (clothing, bakery and butcher interiors, town hall, police, cafe) read well. Night is lost in three ways:

1. **Interiors with no lamp at all** (gun store, hostel, pub, fuel shop, newsagent kiosk, registry): pitch black where a player does business.
2. **Shop fascias that are dark plates at night** (bank, clinic, bakery, butcher, supermarket, pharmacy, furniture, garage, pub): the sign is unreadable even when the shop inside is bright.
3. **Fronts with no street light** (pharmacy, furniture barn, garage, car lot, bank, bus stop b32): the door and the stop cannot be found.

**Not graded.** The camera ended on the roof for the supermarket interior (the anchor was a ceiling lamp), in a neighbouring flat for the clinic desk, and behind the market price board; the bank teller counter, the truck-yard examiner hut and the exterior of the general store and cafe have no frame. A fix card re-shoots those stops as its proof. The HUD quick-slots chip shows in some frames; the world shots are otherwise clean.

## Fix cards

Three cards, each with its own files. None edits the same file as another. Each re-shoots its stops at 22:00 in a session as its proof (`time 22`, `capture world <name> x y z ox oy oz 22`).

**Card A: fascia night legibility.** Stops: bank, clinic, supermarket, bakery, butcher, pharmacy, furniture, garage, pub. Give `LF_Sign_Fascia` a night read: a small emissive face or a shaded lamp over the plate, switched with the other night lights. Files: `addons/LifeFramework/Prefabs/Props/Signs/LF_Sign_Fascia.et`, `LF_SignFace.et`, and `addons/LifeFramework/Scripts/Game/Feature/Signs/LF_SignComponent.c`. Proof: the nine fascias, plus the clinic duty desk, supermarket floor and bank teller counter the sweep could not frame.

**Card B: interior lamps in the kits.** Stops: hostel, gun store, pub, fuel shop, newsagent kiosk, registry. Add `LF_LightCeiling_Interior` rows (as the bakery and clinic kits already have) to six kits, then `town fit` them. Files: `tools/town/kits/hostel.json`, `gun-counter.json`, `fuel.json`, `newsagent.json`, `org.json`, `village-bar.json` (the Chotain pub), and the stamped layers `Place_saint-philippe_{hostel,gun-counter,fuel,newsagent,org}.layer`, `Place_chotain_pub.layer`. Proof: the counter, clerk or bed readable in the 22:00 frame of each.

**Card C: exterior lamps and lamp-head light.** Stops: bank front, pharmacy entrance, furniture barn, garage front, car lot, bus stop b32, and lamp head 2's blown-out wall. Add lamps to the night-light set and soften the lamp-head light. Files: `addons/LF_Everon1989/Worlds/MainWorld/MainWorld_Layers/Lights_saint-philippe.layer`, the generator `tools/town/nightlights.mjs`, `addons/LifeFramework/Prefabs/Props/Lights/LF_LampHeadNight.et`. Proof: `night-light-proof` and `audit placement --strict` stay green (no unattached light), and the six fronts read at 22:00.

## Method

`session up --mission island --profile full`, one client; the client signed in through the quiz, then `time 22` (the server sets the clock and the weather to Clear) and a 35 s wait for the 30 s night-light tick. For each view the camera was placed from the layer coordinates (fascia or counter plus 3.5 to 9 m, aimed back at it), the floor taken from the harness `probe ground` for street views, and the shot taken with `capture world ... 22` (HUD off, the clock left to the server). `session down` ran straight after the last shot.
