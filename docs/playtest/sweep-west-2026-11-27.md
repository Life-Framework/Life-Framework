# West and coast sweep, 2026-11-27

The west and coast sites outside the town sweeps of 11-25: the dock (all 8 slots), Fisherman's Bay, Black Lake, the scrapyard, Road Fuel and Road Farm. Method and bar are those of `visual-sweep-2026-11-25.md` and `visual-sweep-night-2026-11-25.md`.

**Result: 13 slots, 9 OK, 4 BELOW, 4 fixed** (one PR per BELOW slot; the Road Fuel fix took two PRs, the second only corrected the lamp heights).

- **Day:** `tools\cli look place:<place>/<slot>` for every slot, in batches of one to four (a batch of eight timed out before the island world opened, twice, while three other sweeps held the Workbench lanes). Pictures: `website/public/docs-images/west-sweep-1127/sweep-day-*.jpg`. No `--interior auto` look was run: the only business done indoors is the wholesale hall, which is open to the quay and was framed from inside in the night session (buyer, iron clerk and price board).
- **Night:** one full-profile island session, `agent-box/west-night-shots.mjs` (new, committed): the clock pinned to 22:00 with the harness `time` verb, a 40 s wait for the 30 s night-light tick, then `capture world` at eye height (target y is the ground plus 1.5 m, camera offset y -1.5). 37 frames in the sweep session, `session down` straight after. Pictures: `sweep-night-*.jpg`. A second island session carried the four fixes together for the after pictures.
- **Bar:** placeholder-looking props, floating, sunk or clipping props, empty counters or shelves, unreadable or missing signs, pitch-black places where a player does business, props on slopes, debug-looking text.
- **Sign faces** are not judged from `look` (a runtime render target). Every pole plate here is the stock prefab; the night frames that show one (Black Lake, smokehouse, warehouse board) read.

## What the lamp fixes taught

- **A street lamp row in a terrain-frame layer has no height of its own.** Written at y 0 it sits below the surface (`look contact`: `NO SUPPORT`, the camera under the ground); at the fit's own y (0.974 at the farm) it floats 0.74 m (the island audit). The ground offset of these spots is about 0.23 in the terrain frame; the farm lamps are at 0.233 (-1.2 and -1.1 cm), the fuel lamps at 0.231 (measured -4.4 cm at 0.187). `town fit` of a slot whose props were hand-seated also moves them, so the lamps were added to the layer by hand at the fit's seats.
- **`LF_LanternGlow` on a lantern prop** is the pattern for places off the lamp grid (north outpost camp, #4978): the glow's measured gap (+27.5 cm) is the lamp head, not a float.
- **`wt ship` needs `origin/main` merged** when main's island placement baseline shrank after the branch was cut (`placement-gate-selftest`: "grew by 3 rows").
