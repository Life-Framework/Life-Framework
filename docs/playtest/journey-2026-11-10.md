## What changed in the harness

- `state` prints `jail`, `garage`, `parked`, `stash`. `stash` and `park` are harness verbs
  (the inventory drag and driving have no harness path); `apples` moves behind the harness gate.
- The restart judge reads the server log for a loaded sentence because a 60 s sentence can run out
  before the clients are back.
- The report converts the engine's BMP captures to PNG (they were copied as BMPs named `.png`).
- Every new player is granted a stored S105 and an impounded UAZ, so `garage` starts at 1.
