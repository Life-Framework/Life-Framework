# East industry sweep, 2026-11-27

The six east industry sites that miners, smelters and haulers use: the East compound smelter, the East Rise coal seam, the Highstone iron mine, Coalman's Brow, the power plant coal buyer and the NE compound truck yard with its examiner. Each was shot by day with `tools\cli look place:<place>/<slot>` (none has an enterable shop room, so no `--interior` run: the business is outdoors at a counter, a rock, a furnace or a pole plate), then at 22:00 in one full-profile island session (`agent-box/east-night-shots.mjs`: the clock pinned with the harness `time` verb, `stand` at the site, `capture world` at eye height from the probed ground, `session down` straight after). The same session script re-shot the sites after the fixes. Pictures are in `website/public/docs-images/east-night-1127/`.

**Result: 2 OK, 4 BELOW, 4 fixed** (6 slots). By day all six are OK. At 22:00 the smelter and the three mines were pitch black: their lanterns were plain meshes with no light, so a player could not find the sign, the furnace or the ore. Each BELOW got its own `town-draft-<place>-<slot>-night` PR; the 22:00 after pictures below are from one session with all four layers loaded.

The method and the bar are those of `docs/playtest/visual-sweep-2026-11-25.md` and `visual-sweep-night-2026-11-25.md`: placeholder-looking props, floating, sunk or clipping props, unreadable or missing signs, pitch-black work sites or counters, props on slopes. Not taste. A sign face is only judged in the session (a Workbench `look` draws it blank).

## Notes

- **Why the fix is stool lamps.** The kit lanterns were `LampKerosene_01` and `MiningLantern_01` meshes with no light, many lying in tall grass. The core `LF_LanternGlow.et` (#4980, warm, LV 4, 7 m, no shadow, 18:00 to 06:00) gives each lamp a visible source; a lamp on a vanilla `Stool_01` stands clear of the grass and its pool of light falls on the ground and the ore. By day only the lamps and stools show.
- **A name trap.** `tools/actions/counter-npc.mjs` reads `counter` in a prop name as a counter NPC anchor, so a Coalman's Brow glow named `*_counter` failed `validate-action-reach`; the shop-side lamps are named `*_shop`.
- **Seating.** The placement audit and the contact sheets read three stools 1.2 to 2.6 cm sunk on the first pass; each was raised 1.5 to 3.5 cm. The pictures here were taken before those nudges (the lamps barely moved), so the final seating is the contact sheet in each PR. `look` draws sign faces blank, so the plates are judged only from the session pictures.
- **Not changed.** The power plant buyer and the truck yard were re-shot as controls at 22:00 and read as before.
- **Method files.** `agent-box/east-night-shots.mjs` (the shot list, `before` or `after`); the flow `island-outpost-night` signs the client in.
