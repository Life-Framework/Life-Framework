## Findings

**Real player-hitting game bugs: none.** No issue filed, no milestone touched.

1. **`drive-haul-proof` fails its three driven legs** (1a truck to a tree: stuck 33.4 m in 136.7 s; 2a the loaded truck back to the saw: stuck 36.2 m in 147.8 s, 20.3 m off the pad, 2 backups; 3a saw to the timber counter: stuck 28.9 m in 135.9 s, 187.1 m off the pad). **Harness limit**, the one from dry run 3. The truck moves this time (dry run 4: 0 to 4 m), but never reaches a target. Every economy step around the legs passes (1b to 1d felling, loading and 6 logs 12/12 units, 2b 6 of 6 sawn, 2c +$180 at the woodlot buyers, 3b +$15 at the timber counter, **+$195 in 15.5 min, $753/h**; `economy` says steady $742/h), and `truck-haul-proof` (the same haul with the truck moved by the proof) passes 12 of 12 in the same session. The manual drive in "Manual testing" stays the only way to rule out a player-side problem.
2. **`booking-desk-proof` failed once on its frame-gap check, then passed.** Step 1f0 reads the longest wall time between two frames on the suspect's client B while B pays (#4824 froze it for about 20 s; the check fails at 1 s). Run 1: 3616 ms. Rerun in the same session, same two clients: 533 ms, all 24 steps. Not a repeatable freeze (dry run 4 passed it as well), and the PC was carrying two other agents' sessions with 0.5 GB of VRAM free at the time, so a client stall from load is the likelier cause than a regression of #4824. It is not a proof defect either, because the check is the one asked for. Not filed: no repro. If Saturday's run shows it again on an idle box, that is a player-hitting hitch on pay and gets an issue then.
3. **The updated order works**: `starter-gates-proof` ran straight after the flow on the same character and passed (50.6 s), the #5122 change doing what the page says. `session up` booked every session on the first try (no RAM refusal this time; the box had 17 to 26 GB free).
