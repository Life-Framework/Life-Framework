# M1 release candidate QA, 2026-11-08

The M1 journey played as a stranger, on dedicated servers with game clients at 1920x1080: this PC (hidden desktop) and the test laptop `lf-testbox` (its signed-in desktop, through the interactive client launch in #200). Bar: a stranger finishes the loop with no help from outside the game, and every step was looked at, not inferred from a log.

Verdict: **not ready yet.** The blockers found in this pass are fixed and merged (below). Still open: a green check of the jail move on a real client, the focused-client audio pass, and a restart round trip for the org, stash, car, jail time, and tickets. The maintainer's own `tools\cli playtest` run on current `main` is the next step.
