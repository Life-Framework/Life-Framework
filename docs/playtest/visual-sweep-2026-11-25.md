# Visual sweep, 2026-11-25

Every player-facing Saint-Philippe and island site outside yesterday's sweep (supermarket, butcher, bakery, Chotain pub, garage and respray bay, clinic sign, Coalman's Brow, power plant coal buyer, smelter, pharmacy, fish stall, mine bench, north outpost, shift board, barber, flag wall), shot with `tools\cli look place:<place>/<slot>` and, for interiors, `--interior auto`. Contact sheets for every graded building and yard were read in full. Marker-only slots whose own sheet was not opened (anniversary, fuel and parcel drops, payphones, safe zone, dock raid and shipment) were judged from the neighbouring sheets that show them, and the church, range, market, wholesale, warehouse, smokehouse, fuel and parcel interiors were not opened (the exterior and kit close-ups carried the grade). Pictures are in `website/public/docs-images/visual-sweep-1125/`.

**Result: 58 OK, 8 BELOW** (66 site rows; the first pass said 37 OK, 29 BELOW before the in-game sign check below). Nothing was fixed in this PR.

**The blank signs were a `look` artifact, not a defect.** The first pass read 21 rows as BELOW because a sign plate shows blank white in a Workbench `look`: a sign face is a render-target texture painted at runtime, and `look` never draws it. 45 of the 50 `LF_SignDef` rows have no `m_aRows` and no `m_sStyle` on purpose; the stock pole plate (`LF_Sign_PolePlate`) prints the def's title in its painted face (`LF_SignComponent.FaceText`). A full-profile island session (2026-11-25) stood at nine of these signs with the harness `stand` and `shot world` verbs, at eye height, and every one reads: Highstone, East Rise, Black Lake, the truck yard gate, a house for sale, the ATM plate, the labour desk, the registry and the town rules, with the respray bay as the control. 9 of 9 rendered, 0 blank. Rows marked OK without a walk use the same prefabs. A sign's face is only judged in a session, never from a `look` picture.
