# M2 release QA, 2026-11-10

M2 Living Town played end to end on the playtest town, on fresh `main` after #463 (journey), #466 (debt and
sirens), #478 (physical store) and #489 (square payphone) merged. Dedicated server, this PC, three game
clients (all three joined every time). The driver is player-equivalent: `use`, `stand`, the screen's own
buttons, `state` before and after, and the server log line as the verdict.

## Verdict: READY for a guided M2 playtest

43 of 51 systems pass, 1 failed and was fixed in this change, 7 were not run because only a human
can drive them. The journey passes 21 of 21 on three clients, with a restart that keeps rank, stash, the
stored car and a running sentence. Nothing found blocks a playtest. It does **not** prove M2: the human-only
list below is where the next bugs are, and one regression (below) got past every merge because no test asked
whether the pick action is *shown* on a car.

## Bugs found

Fixed in this change:

- **Lockpick on a car never offered** (#412, regression). #457 (lock-start) resolved a merge by putting the
  door-only check back into `LF_LockpickAction.CanBeShownScript`, so a locked car never listed the pick action.
  `hotwire-proof` failed on fresh main with `cannot_show`. The WORLD test `e2e/lockpick-vehicle` now asserts the
  action shows on the car (red observed: `- the lockpick action shows on the locked car`; green after the fix).
- **`store-proof` could not run**: the registry split (#486) left its import list empty.

Filed in M2 (label `m2`):

- #493 the death screen title is clipped ("You are do").
- #494 org ledger: the lease status is cut off, and the interaction prompt draws over the open ledger.
- #495 a reported stolen car lists the thief at "wanted level 0 of 5" on the board.
- #496 agent proofs leave duty, wanted and downed state behind (25 proofs in one session fail from the second on;
  each needs a fresh session). `bill-proof` says playtest but passes only on the debug mission.
- #497 visual: the moonshine still renders as a plain white boiler.

Seen and not filed: the first-person capture sits inside the head, so the store shots show the player's hair
across the frame.

## Journey

`session.mjs up --clients 3 --mission playtest`, `roster 2`, then `node agent-box/journey.mjs`: 21 of 21 (arrive on
three clients, job, earn, ATM, shop, vehicle, give, clock in, rob, wanted board, dispatch, ticket, org, org C,
hideout, stash, contraband, garage, arrest, jail, restart). It passed on the branch before the late merges and again
after #466, #478 and #489 landed. Report: `agent-box/reports/journey.html` (local, gitignored). The store is
covered by `store-proof`, not a journey step.

## Run notes

- Many proofs are not independent: a proof that clocks in leaves the officer on duty and the next one clocks them
  out. Early in the run about ten proofs "failed" for that reason and each passed alone (#496). Only fresh-session
  results are counted above.
- `siren-late-proof` (the live check #486 needed) passed on the debug mission with one client and a late joiner.
- The square payphone (#489) offers all five call actions where a player stands and the dial goes through; with
  nobody on duty it answers `NoAnswer`, as designed.
- Pending PRs: none. #202 and #464 landed (#478, #466) and are in the table.

## Human-only checks

What the harness cannot do, or cannot judge:

- **Real key presses**: F on each interaction, hold X to give up, the inventory drag into a stash, aiming a weapon.
- **Animations**: carry and drag, hands-up on hold-up, the payphone and board gestures, the wake animation.
- **Sound**: payphone coin and dial tone, pager beep, siren, store and site alarms, the scanner chirp, radio.
- **Driving**: the bought car to the garage, the stolen car to the chop shop, an ambulance delivery by driving
  (the harness sets the car down or seats the thief).
- **Three-player feel**: crowding and queueing at the till and the board, two officers taking one call.
- **Dedicated server with real latency**: every run here was local; joiners over a real link, a restart with
  players connected from other machines.
- **Game Master**: the incident panel (#240), admin actions (#246), `!handled`, `!wadd`.
- **Moderation by hand**: hold-up (#244), aim intent (#245).
- **Medic and evidence by hand**: first aid items (#257), casings after live fire (#268), questioning an NPC
  witness in person (#267).
- **Death flow**: the wristband toast after the R-89 form on a new life (#264; the shot is hidden by the form),
  the clipped death title (#493).
- **Visual passes**: the moonshine still (#497), the store counter and shelves, the cell yard.
