**Town rules**
Life Framework | Everon 1989 (Alpha). You are a person with a life here, not a soldier. These rules hold for everyone in town.

1. No random violence: hurt someone only for a reason your character has.
2. Fear for your life: with a gun on you, comply, then report it.
3. New life: after a death, forget what led to it. Do not hunt the killer.
4. Keep disputes out of the streets. No stalking, no harassing.
5. No combat logging: play out an arrest, a robbery or a fight.

Take a complaint to staff after the scene, not during it. This is an alpha: the world can be wiped, with 24 hours' notice.
