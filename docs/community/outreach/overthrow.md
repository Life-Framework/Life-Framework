# Outreach: the Overthrow community

Status: not asked

Overthrow gets a credit-first post. We use some of its code with the author's
permission, and this community owes us nothing. The post says thanks, says what
we took and what we are doing differently, and does not compete for players.

## Community

Overthrow (github.com/ArmaOverthrow/Overthrow.Arma4) is a resistance and
occupation campaign mod for Reforger with its own community. Its licence is APL-SA;
the Overthrow owner gave Life Framework permission to use its code and relicense it
under our MIT licence, with credit (`docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-08;
`THIRD_PARTY.md`; `docs/overthrow-survey.md`). The maintainer holds the record of
that permission.

What we took: four small files, listed in `THIRD_PARTY.md`: money formatting
and delta tracking, resource definitions, and shop rules. Each carries the
Overthrow header. We did not port its campaign, AI war or map. See the
compatibility decision: "do not collide", no bridge or importer.

## Rules

UNVERIFIED. We could not read this community's rules from outside it. Read them
and the channel list first. Overthrow's own server may keep promotion to one
channel or none.

## Where

Ask first. Message the author or a mod before anything is posted, even though
the permission exists: it is the author's community and the post will be read as
about them. If they prefer a different place, or nothing, follow that.

## Ask the mods

```
Hi. I'm the maintainer of Life Framework, an open-source life and roleplay
framework for Arma Reforger. With the author's permission we use a few small
files from Overthrow (money formatting, resource definitions, shop rules),
credited in the repo and relicensed MIT as agreed. Thank you for that.

Would you be happy for me to post a short thank-you note in <channel> saying
what we used, and that we're a different kind of mod (a life and roleplay
framework, not a campaign)? I'd post it once with one link, and I'm happy to
show you the text first. If you'd rather I didn't, no problem.
```

## Post

About 650 characters. Show it to the author or a mod before posting.

```
Thank you to the Overthrow author. Life Framework, an open-source life and roleplay framework for Arma Reforger (early test build), uses a few small files from Overthrow with the author's permission: money formatting, resource definitions and shop rules. They keep Overthrow's header and are listed in THIRD_PARTY.md in our repo.

We aren't doing what Overthrow does. It's a resistance campaign; we're a life and roleplay framework: jobs, police, medics and crime in a small 1989 Everon town, with interactions in the world instead of menus. No campaign, no AI war, no bridge between the two mods.

It's built mostly by AI agents under my direction and there's no release date. If you're curious: https://life-framework.org/join/overthrow
```

## After

- "Are you stealing code?" Answer from `THIRD_PARTY.md` and the decisions entry. Quote: the owner gave explicit permission to use the code and relicense it under MIT, with credit. Do not argue the licence. Pass any licence challenge to the maintainer.
- "Will you work with Overthrow?" There is no bridge, and none is planned unless a real need appears (`docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-09). Say so.
- "Is this a competing mod?" No: different mod, different goal.
