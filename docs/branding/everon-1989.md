# Brand brief: Life Framework: Everon 1989

DRAFT for the maintainer's sign-off (#375). Agents write the Workshop kit, the in-game
briefing (#374), the loading screen and the website from this page. Decision:
`docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-09 "Two Workshop items, two brands".

## Who it is for

Players, and the server owners who want a ready flagship server. The page never explains the
engine.

## Premise

Everon, 1989. You have just arrived in a village where everyone knows everyone and nobody has
told you the rules. You fill in form R-89, collect an ID card from the clerk on the square and
find your feet. Honest work pays: parcels, fuel, fares. The bank keeps your money and the
police keep order. At the edge of the village, a few people make more on a good night than on
a good week, and the police would like a word.

You are one more person trying to get by. Nothing in the setting says which way you go.

This is a premise, not a plot. There is no chosen hero, no campaign and no dated event.

## Voice

Period and human, still plain. Second person, present tense. Short sentences. Concrete things
(a form, a counter, a till) over adjectives. No game jargon (XP, quest, NPC), no modern slang,
no winks at the player. Never "tech demo" or "playtest town" in player-facing copy once the
briefing (#374) lands; until then the scenario keeps its current name.

## Art direction

Late-afternoon light, 1989 period vehicles and signs, worn paint, paper notices, cash and cards
rather than screens. Photographed in the game with the in-game camera, no HUD, no text or logo
baked into the image. Distinct from the core preview: this one shows a place and the people in
it, not the framework. Era rules from the concept work apply: show what a 1989 local actually
used, not an antique or rustic version.
