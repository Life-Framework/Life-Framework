# 5. Shops

Every shop in Saint-Philippe has a counter and a clerk. Press **F** at the counter and pick the row you want. Shops take **cash**
and their prices are fixed; the till sells what is listed here.
The numbers in the first column are the numbers on the [Saint-Philippe map](maps.md).

| Where | What |
|---|---|

| **Gun store** | Firearm permit **$250** (instant in the alpha). Weapons need the permit |
| **Car lot** | Six cars, $560 to $3,200 at the alpha price. Paid from the bank. A driving license is needed |

## What the clothing store and the gun store carry

* **Clothing store:** 65 items, with a rack and a workwear counter. Shirts $25 to $30, trousers $35 to $170, jackets $30 to $250, three raincoat colors $60, 26 hats and caps $15 to $38 (flat caps, knitted hats, trucker caps, berets, ushanka, hard hats), work boots $40 and $48, gloves $15 to $20, watches $35 and $45, and rucksacks priced by size: canvas sack $28, Swiss M70 $30, Kolobok $60, ALICE $80, field pack $110. No military uniforms; the Rally gear belongs to a game DLC the shop cannot sell. The dressed mannequins sell what they wear: scroll on one for each piece and its price, or the whole outfit at the sum, and press F; if your pockets are full the piece is left at your feet.
* **Gun store:** Pistol PM $350 and Rifle M21 $1,500 (a permit is needed); magazines $15 to $35; scopes $300 and $450; holsters $25 and $30; a magazine pouch $25; the gang radio $60. Scopes, holsters and pouches are sold to anyone. The vz. 58 and the M9 are not on the counter (the vz. 58 is a black market rifle at $1,800). The self-serve shelf sells a ushanka at $38, the same price as the clothing store.
* **Item names:** every shop row has a plain name. The hardware store lists an *Entrenching tool*, the pharmacy *Aspirin*, *Gauze roll*, *Sterile gauze pack*, *Buckle tourniquet* and *Rubber tourniquet*, the farm supply a *Reaper* and the clothing rack names each color and variant (*Cotton shirt, red plaid*; *Ushanka, flaps up* and *flaps down*; *Wristwatch, brown strap*). A shop or car lot price list shows six to eight rows at a time, and each lot titles its own list, for example *Truck yard - price list*.
