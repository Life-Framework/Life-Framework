# 5. Shops

Every shop in Saint-Philippe has a counter and a clerk. Press **F** at the counter and pick the row you want. Shops take **cash**
and their prices are fixed; the till sells what is listed here.
The numbers in the first column are the numbers on the [Saint-Philippe map](maps.md).

## Services you buy at a counter

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Town hall** (town clerk) | Driving licence **$100** |
| **Gun store** | Firearm permit **$250** (instant in the alpha). Weapons need the permit |
| **Car lot** | Seven cars, $560 to $6,400 at the alpha price. Paid from the bank. A driving licence is needed |
| **Garage** | Car parts **$200**, repair kit **$80**. Buys scrap metal **$4**, copper wire **$12**, rubber scrap **$5** |
| **Filling station** pumps | **$2 per litre**. Pick *Fill the tank* with your car at the pump |
| **Barber** | A haircut |
| **Cafe** | Drinks and snacks; chess table beside it |
| **Market stall** | Buys your apple juice, cabbage, honey and planks: look at the stall and scroll, one **Sell** row per good you carry with its price, plus **Sell all goods**. No inventory window |
| **Dock wholesale yard** | Buys planks, boards, posts, firewood, cement, coal and metal bars in bulk; price falls as you sell and recovers hourly. Also buys every fish and smoked fish at a fixed price (a trout is $8, a smoked tuna $43; see [Jobs](03-jobs.md#fishing)) |
| **Timber counter** | Buys planks **$8** and hardwood boards **$12** (a purse that refills through the hour) |

## What the clothing store and the gun store carry

* **Clothing store:** 65 items, with a rack and a workwear counter. Shirts $25 to $30, trousers $35 to $170, jackets $38 to $250, three raincoat colours $60, 26 hats and caps $15 to $38 (flat caps, knitted hats, trucker caps, berets, ushanka, hard hats), work boots $48, gloves $15 to $20, watches $35 and $45, and rucksacks priced by size: canvas sack $28, Swiss M70 $30, Kolobok $60, ALICE $80, field pack $110. No military uniforms; the Rally gear belongs to a game DLC the shop cannot sell. The dressed mannequins sell what they wear: scroll on one for each piece and its price, or the whole outfit at the sum, and press F; if your pockets are full the piece is left at your feet.
* **Gun store:** Pistol PM $350, Pistol M9 $500, Rifle M21 $1,500, rifle VZ-58 $1,800 (a permit is needed); magazines $15 to $40; scopes $300 and $450; holsters $25 to $30.
