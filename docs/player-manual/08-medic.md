# 8. Medic and clinic

## When you are shot

A lethal hit **knocks you down**; it does not kill you. While you are down you cannot move or act and take no more damage. Other players can **search** you.

* You have **3 minutes** before you bleed out.
* A **bandage** adds 30 seconds, a **tourniquet** 45 seconds and a **saline bag** 60 seconds. Anyone can use them on you. Morphine does not add time.
* A **medic on duty** can **Revive** you if they reach you in time (within 2.5 m). A medic or an on-duty officer can **load you into a car** to get you to the clinic when a vehicle with a free seat is next to you.
* After **30 seconds** you can **give up**: hold **X** (gamepad **Y**). That counts as death. Logging out while down also counts.

## After dying

You **wake in a hospital bed** in the clinic. A **paper bill** for **$150** is put in your pack. Pay it at the **hospital till**: your money in the bank is never touched. What you carried **stays with your body** where you died.
A revive by a medic costs **$100** (half of it goes to the medic).

## The clinic

The **clinic** (map name *Clinic*, sign *Everon Village Hospital*) is the converted town house next to the taxi rank, just north of the main street.
It has a waiting room, a **reception counter**, two **hospital beds**, the **medic duty desk** and a **medicine cabinet**.
The **pharmacy** north of the clinic sells aspirin ($10), gauze ($8) and tourniquets ($25) over the counter; saline bags ($60) and morphine ($80, medics only) come from the pharmacy counter.

## Working as a medic

1. Go to the **medic duty desk** in the clinic and press **F** to **Clock in**. Anyone may sign on; there is no roster. Your civilian things are stored and you take the medic kit.
2. Wait at the clinic or patrol. The **medic dispatch board** lists calls. Revive downed players and bring patients in.
3. **Clock out** at the same desk. You are **paid in cash** for the time you were on duty.

| Rank | Pay per hour | Bonus cap per hour | Adds to your kit | On duty to rise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orderly | $480 | $40 | Dressing, tourniquet | 1 hour |
| EMT | $500 | $50 | Saline bag | 2 hours |
| Paramedic | $530 | $50 | Morphine | 4 hours |
| Doctor | $560 | $40 | Surgical gauze | top |

Reviving and delivering patients earns **medic job experience** and a **bonus**: **$10** a revive and **$15** a patient put in a hospital bed (the bed's own revive is included, so a bed delivery is $15, not $25). Reviving yourself earns nothing. The bonus stops at your rank's cap each hour and is paid with your shift when you clock out.

**Ambulance pool board.** On duty, press **F** at the ambulance pool board and pick your rank's ambulance ("Take the Orderly ambulance": the white UAZ452 with AMBULANCE on the sides, Orderly and EMT; "Take the Paramedic ambulance": the white S1203, Paramedic and Doctor). It stands in the bay in front of the board, unlocked for medics on duty only. Taking another one sends your last back, unless someone is sitting in it. The board lists only the ambulances your rank may take.

The clinic, the taxi rank, the pharmacy and the notice board are on the [Saint-Philippe map](maps.md).

**When the clinic is short.** Break into the clinic's drug cabinet and the pharmacy runs out of stock for a while. A paper notice appears beside the clinic's front door ("Pharmacy closed: Stock stolen. Medicine: Prices raised. Until: Further notice"), the counter charges more and a revive near the clinic takes longer. The notice comes down when the clinic is stocked again.
