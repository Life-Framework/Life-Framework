# 9. Controls

Walking, aiming, driving, the inventory and the map use the **normal Arma Reforger controls**. Life Framework adds the keys below.
You can change every one of them in **Settings, Controls, Life Framework**.

## Everyday

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| **Use** a counter, sign, door, ATM, bed or any prompt, then pick the row | **F** | the usual Reforger interact button |
| Open the **map** | **M** | the usual Reforger map button |
| **Set a waypoint** (map open) | **Shift + Left click** on the map, or click a place | hold **Y**, press **A** (or **A** on a place) |
| **Clear the waypoint** (map open) | **Backspace**, or Shift + Left click on it | double-tap **Y** |
| Fold the **how to use the map** card (map open) | tap **F1** | click the **left stick** |
| Fold the **key** of icons (map open) | click the **Key** tab, top right | |
| **Throw** the item in your hand (a tin, a bottle) | **G** | hold **right bumper**, press **B** |
| **Hands up** (to a police officer) | **F7** | **right bumper + X** |
| **Emote menu** | **F1** | |
| **Give up** while downed (hold, after 30 seconds) | hold **X** | hold **Y** |
| Mark a moment for a **clip** | **F8** | **right bumper + View** |
| Open the **welcome guide** again | **F3** | **right bumper + Y** |
| Open your **personal file** (money, papers, skills, errands) | **F2** | hold **right bumper + Y** |

## Vehicles

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| **Lock or unlock** your car | **F6** | press the **right stick** |
| Cycle the **siren** modes (police car, driver seat) | right mouse button (click or double click) | click the **left stick** |

## Inventory

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| **Use** food or a drink | pick the item and press **Use** | same |
| **Split** a stack | right mouse button | **Menu** |
| **Quick split** | hold the right mouse button | hold **Menu** |
| **Move a quantity** | hold **Left Alt** | hold **X** |

## Talking to people

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Pick an answer | the number shown beside it (**1** to **4**) | left stick up or down, then **A** |
| Say **goodbye** and end the talk | **0** (the last row is always "0. Goodbye"), or **Esc** | **B** |
| Skip what they are saying | **Enter** | **A** |

## Keypads (ATM)

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Type a number | **0 to 9** (number row or numpad) | move to a key and press it |
| Delete a digit | **Backspace** | **X** |
| Clear | **Delete** | **Y** |
| Leave the ATM | **Esc** | **B** |

## Building (house plots and furniture)

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| **Place** | **Enter** | **right trigger** |
| **Cancel** | **Backspace** | **B** |
| **Turn** left, right | **[** and **]** | **left trigger**, **right bumper** |
| **Fine** movement | hold **Left Shift** | hold the **left stick** |

## Police, medic and fishing

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Cuff, uncuff | hold the prompt in the player menu (about 2 seconds); handcuffs in your left hand |
| Search, arrest, write ticket | the player menu: press **F** on the player |
| Revive | the player menu on a downed player, medic on duty |
| Cast and strike with the fishing rod | rod in hand, **fire** to cast, **fire** again when the float goes under |
| Fell a tree | axe in hand, use the prompt on the tree |

The **F10** key opens a developer menu that works only on a server that turns it on. You will not use it.

## Admin commands (server staff only)

An administrator types these in the chat after `#login <admin password>`. Anyone else is refused.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `#lfmoney <player id or name> <amount>` | Give cash to a player who is online; a negative amount takes cash back. Up to $1,000,000 a call |
| `#lfmoney <player id or name> <amount> bank` | The same, on the bank balance |

The amount is saved like any other change to a balance. `#players` shows the player ids.
