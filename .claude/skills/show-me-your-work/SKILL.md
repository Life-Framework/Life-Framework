# Decision Trail

For each consequential decision, record one short line containing:
`decision | evidence | rejected option | status`.

Use a local trail for work spanning phases or sessions. Commit it only when the
reasoning must survive independently of the diff. Point every verification
claim at a test, log, or runtime observation. End with done, proven, and next.
